# Sources (checked Oct 19, 2026)

## Demand / survey data
- Menlo Ventures, 2026 State of Consumer AI (PDF): https://menlovc.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/menlo_ventures_consumer_ai_report-2026.pdf
  - 41% of AI users have tried an AI agent; 24% use one regularly; 72% aware.
  - Among shoppers who use AI: 50% compare prices, 26% let AI complete a purchase.
- Menlo Ventures, 2025 State of Consumer AI: https://menlovc.com/perspective/2025-the-state-of-consumer-ai/
  - 61% of US adults used AI in past 6 months; 19% daily; only 3% pay. Top uses: emails 19%, to-do lists 18%, meal planning 16%, expenses 15%, health questions 14%.
- Channel V Media AI Barometer (Prosper Insights, June 2026, n=7,675), via Search Engine Watch: https://searchenginewatch.com/four-in-ten-americans-would-hand-ai-a-chore-survey-finds/
  - 40.1% would let an agent handle at least one of 13 tasks; 7.7% already use agentic AI; restaurant booking 12.1%, groceries 11%, car buying 5%; only 17.6% think agents are a good idea, 40.6% unsure.
- EY AI Sentiment Study wave 4 (2026): https://www.ey.com/content/dam/ey-unified-site/ey-com/en-gl/insights/ai/documents/ey-gl-ai-sentiment-study-wave-04-2026.pdf
  - 36% would prefer AI auto-applies discounts; 34% AI contacts customer service to fix issues; 21% AI books doctor's appointments; 16% used AI that acted on their behalf in last 6 months.
- Visa Business & Economic Insights, Feb 2026 (Canada): https://usa.visa.com/content/dam/VCOM/regional/na/us/partner-with-us/economic-insights/documents/vbei-feb-2026-can-consumer-insights.pdf
  - Restaurant 18%, groceries 17%, healthcare 14%, travel 14%; 34% want human review over a set amount; 29% want a "panic button".
- KFF, Claims Denials and Appeals in ACA Marketplace Plans in 2024 (Mar 24, 2026): https://www.kff.org/patient-consumer-protections/claims-denials-and-appeals-in-aca-marketplace-plans-in-2024/
  - 19% of in-network claims denied; <1% of denied claims appealed; 66% of internal appeals upheld; 33% of insured adults had a denial in past 2 years (KFF poll Jan 2026).
