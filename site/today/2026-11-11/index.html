What changed in AI assistants, what it means for you, and one job to try. About 3 minutes, every weekday.

3 things that changed

  1. 1
    xAxAI✨ featureYou use this

    Grok Bot can now join your Google Meet, Zoom or Teams call, answer questions in the chat and send a summary after

    What it means for you: Paste a meeting link into your Bot's chat and ask it to join; the host lets it in, and it keeps track of decisions, answers questions in the meeting chat and sends notes and action items afterward. It's for Team, Enterprise and Ultra plans, and you should tell everyone on the call that a bot is listening.

    Source: Grok Bot changelog · Also: Grok Bot docs: Add a Bot to your meetings · 7.1K likes on X

    ⚠ Touches Grok Bot: check your settings →
  2. 2
    QwAlibaba🧠 model releaseYou use this

    Alibaba says Qwen4 is coming and opens a two-week preview of its top model, Qwen4-Max

    What it means for you: Qwen4-Max is Alibaba's new flagship for coding, office work and agent tasks, and it can read images and video, search the web and use tools with a 1-million-token memory. For now it's only a preview through Alibaba's Token Plan subscription and a few of its own apps (Qwen app, QwenWork, Qoder, QwenStudio); the preview runs until Nov 25 and there's no pay-as-you-go price yet.

    Source: Alibaba Cloud · 12.7K likes on X

  3. 3
    GGoogle🧠 model releaseYou use this

    Google's new Gemini Decision 1 model answers up to 1,000 yes/no or pick-one questions in a fraction of a second

    What it means for you: Instead of writing a reply, it scores fixed choices (is this email urgent, which tool should the bot use, does this photo break a rule) and returns how confident it is in about 150-250 milliseconds. It's a developer tool in Google AI Studio at $0.10 per million input tokens with nothing charged for output, so bot builders can make cheap, fast sorting and routing steps without paying a big model to think.

    Source: Google blog · 10.6K likes on X

🧰 Job of the day New on the site

Hand your agent a quick errand from your smartwatch

Meta's Muse agent now has an Apple Watch and Wear OS app. Say or type a quick errand on your wrist, like finding movie tickets or fixing a calendar clash, and approve the result without pulling out your phone.

✈️ Bookings & travelEasyWorks in: Meta MuseGemini

You end up with: Small errands started from your wrist and finished in the background, with you approving the final step.

Copy today's prompt 
[Say this to your agent from the watch] Find two tickets for [movie] near [area] this [day] after [time]. Check my calendar for clashes first. Show me the best two options and wait for me to approve before booking or paying.

Watch out: The watch is only a remote: the agent works in the cloud with whatever accounts you connected, so connect as few as you need (Gadgets & Wearables).
