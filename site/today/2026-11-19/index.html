3 things that changed

  1. 1
    OAOpenAI✨ featureYou use this

    OpenAI starts testing dots, ChatGPT's always-on agent, with a small number of Plus users, and brings them to all Business Standard users

    What it means for you: If you pay for ChatGPT Plus you might see a dot appear, the background agent that keeps working on your tasks and goals across connected apps, which until now needed Pro or a higher business plan. It's a limited test, OpenAI hasn't said what Plus usage limits will be, and Pro users with a dot can also switch on a redesigned mobile app where the dot gets its own tab.

    Source: 9to5Mac · Also: The Verge (new mobile design) · 10.7K likes on X

  2. 2
    GGoogle🤖 agent launchYou use this

    Gemini's Mac app can now click, type and work through your apps and websites for you in a background window

    What it means for you: Describe a chore, like copying details from PDFs into a shipping form or staging resale listings from a folder of photos, and Gemini does the clicking in a separate window while you keep using your Mac. You pick which apps it can touch and approve key steps; it's rolling out in English in the US for Google AI Plus, Pro and Ultra subscribers on Apple Silicon Macs (macOS 15+).

    Source: Google · Also: TechCrunch · 2.6K likes on X

    ⚠ Touches Gemini Tasks: check your settings →
  3. 3
    MMeta✨ featureYou use this

    Every Muse user in supported countries can now get a @muse.ai email address their agent sends and receives from

    What it means for you: You can forward Muse an email or ask it to write to a restaurant or a store for you, and it asks your approval before sending; the address doesn't give Muse access to your own inbox. Handles are first come, first served and can't be changed later, and anything you forward may include other people's details, so check the thread before you send it on.

    Source: Meta Help Center · Also: The Tech Outlook · 3.6K likes on X

🧰 Job of the day New on the site

Get your car booked in for a service by letting an AI call the nearby shops

Tell your AI assistant your car is due for a service and when you're free. It phones the nearest shops or dealerships at the same time, sits on hold for you, finds one that takes appointments at a time that fits, and puts it on your calendar.

✈️ Bookings & travelEasyWorks in: Tasks in GeminiMeta MuseGrok Bot

You end up with: A confirmed service appointment on your calendar, plus a short note of what each shop offered, without spending time on hold.

Copy today's prompt 
My [2019 Honda Odyssey] is due for [its regular service / a check-engine light check]. Please call the [two] nearest [dealerships / repair shops] near [my home / ZIP code] and find an appointment on [a weekday morning before 9am] in the next [4 weeks].

- Say you're an AI assistant calling for me.
- Only pick a time when my calendar is free, and skip shops that are walk-in only.
- Ask roughly how long it takes and whether they can give me a ride or a loaner.
- Don't agree to any repairs, prices or deposits, and don't give out my card or account details.
- Before you book, tell me what each shop offered and wait for my OK. Then add the appointment to my calendar with the address.

Watch out: Some businesses won't talk to an AI or hang up, and Google says businesses can opt out of Gemini calls. Have a backup plan if no one picks up.
