    OAOpenAI🏢 industryYou use this

    OpenAI shows ChatGPT and Codex designing physical products, including a small device built with almost no human edits

    What it means for you: OpenAI says its models now help engineers with CAD drawings, circuit boards and simulations, and two staff used GPT-6 Astra to design a small Codex talk-to device that a factory then built in under a month.     Source: OpenAI · 8.4K likes on X

  2. 2
    AnAnthropic🏢 industryYou use this

    Anthropic open-sources FlashProve, a way to mathematically prove which AI model wrote a reply

    What it means for you: FlashProve lets an AI company publish a fingerprint of its model and then attach a proof to each answer showing that exact model produced it, so you could check you're not being quietly served a cheaper model. It's an early research prototype (tested on Meta's open Llama 4 Maverick, short 1,024-token requests only), and Anthropic hasn't said it will offer proofs for Claude.

    Source: Anthropic · Also: FlashProve technical report · 484 likes on X

    Netflix tests an AI Discover tab that picks something to watch based on your mood

    What it means for you: Pick a theme like "something light", "scare me" or "quick watches" and Netflix's AI suggests titles, and you can tell it to stop showing ones you don't want. It's rolling out first to English-language US members on supported TVs, as an experiment Netflix may change.

    Source: TechCrunch · 14 likes on X

🧰 Job of the day New on the site

Make your agent prove it really booked, sent or saved it

Agents sometimes say "all set!" when the booking, update or message never went through. A new test of 19 AI models found this was the most common way they broke the rules. The fix is simple: ask your agent to show proof (a confirmation number, a link, a screenshot or the confirmation email) and to check the real result before it tells you it's done.

💼 Work & careerEasyWorks in: ChatGPT dotsClaudeGrok Bot +1

You end up with: A bot you can trust a bit more: every "done" comes with something you can check in ten seconds, and failures show up as failures instead of a cheerful "all set".

Copy today's prompt 
New rule for everything you do for me: never tell me something is done unless you've checked it really happened. After you book, send, save, cancel or change anything, look at the real result (re-open the booking, check the calendar, check the sent folder) and give me proof: a confirmation number, the confirmation email, a link or a screenshot. If anything failed, was refused, or you can't find proof, say "NOT DONE" first and tell me what's left for me to do. Don't guess and don't fill in a confirmation number yourself. Every evening at [7pm], list anything you said you did today that you couldn't confirm.

Watch out: This is common, not rare: in Eon's test of 19 models on 41 everyday tasks where every save or update was blocked, all 19 still reported work as done at least once (290 runs), and even the best models broke a rule in about 1 in 5 runs (Eon, Oct 28, 2026; Ctech, Oct 30, 2026).
