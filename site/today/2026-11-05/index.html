    OAOpenAI🧠 model releaseYou use this

    OpenAI launches GPT-6 Cyber, a security model that finds and helps fix software bugs

    What it means for you: Approved security teams can use GPT-6 Cyber to hunt for real software holes, check they work, and draft fixes, with stronger rules to stay inside the job they were given. It's only for Daybreak Red customers for now (not everyday ChatGPT), and OpenAI says it already helped find hundreds of Linux and Firefox issues.

    Muse gets real-time voice chat and expressive avatars in the US

    What it means for you: In the Muse app on mobile, web and Mac in the US, tap the phone icon to talk out loud while your Muse's avatar reacts with expressions and gestures, and pick from hundreds of voices. Meta says it's useful for thinking through a trip or rehearsing an interview hands-free, with AI Glasses and Muse Charm support coming later.

    Source: Meta · Also: Meta AI Research, The Verge, TechCrunch · 2K likes on X

    Claude can now read your bank and card accounts and answer money questions (US beta)

Access is read-only so Claude can't move or invest money, chats in the Personal Finance hub aren't used for training, and Anthropic says it isn't designed to give investment advice.

🧰 Job of the day New on the site

Stop your AI chats from being used for training (and check it stays off)

Paying for ChatGPT or Gemini doesn't automatically keep your chats out of model training. This week Hacker News users found Gemini keeps training on saved chats unless you turn off Keep Activity, and some ChatGPT users said the Codex training switch had turned itself back on. Here's how to switch it off and re-check it.

📥 Inbox, calls & paperworkEasyWorks in: ChatGPTGemini

You end up with: Training switched off in each AI app you use, a monthly reminder to re-check, and a habit of using temporary chats for sensitive things.

Copy today's prompt 
Every month on the 1st, remind me: 'Privacy check: ChatGPT > Settings > Data controls > Improve the model for everyone is OFF; Codex settings > Include environments is OFF; Gemini Keep Activity is how I want it.' List the apps I use: [ChatGPT, Gemini, other].

Watch out: Thumbs up/down feedback can send the whole conversation for training even after you opt out, according to OpenAI.
