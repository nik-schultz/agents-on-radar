3 things that changed

  1. 1
    OAOpenAI🏷 pricingYou use this

    OpenAI fixes a GPT-6.2 billing mistake, so paid ChatGPT and Codex users hit limits up to 40% sooner; everyone gets one banked reset

    What it means for you: If you pay for ChatGPT (Go, Plus, Pro or Business) and use GPT-6.2 Astra or Sol, the same work now uses more of your allowance: OpenAI says you'll reach your limits 40% faster on Astra and 25% faster on Sol than last week, because a launch bug had been undercounting them; other models aren't affected and past usage won't be charged. As an apology every Go, Plus, Pro and Business account gets one banked reset, which you redeem in Usage settings on the web or desktop app (not mobile) once an allowance is below 80%, so save it for a day you're about to run out.

    Source: TechRadar · Also: Notebookcheck, OpenAI Help Center (banked resets) · 14.6K likes on X

  2. 2
    OAOpenAI🏷 pricingFrom Nov 25, 2026You use this

    From December, ChatGPT and Codex share one usage allowance on Plus, Pro and Business as Chat and Work merge

    What it means for you: If you pay for ChatGPT Plus, Pro or Business, starting in December your chats, agent work and Codex coding will all draw from one allowance measured in tokens, so long agent or coding jobs can use up what you'd otherwise spend on chats; quick chats with the Instant model stay unlimited and don't count. OpenAI says most people won't wait for resets more often than now, but a spokesperson told TechCrunch a small share of the heaviest users may hit limits sooner; you'll see the combined total and reset times in Settings, then Usage, and a small group of Pro users already has the merged Chat and Work screen.

    Source: The Verge · Also: OpenAI Help Center (usage limits), TechCrunch · 7.7K likes on X

  3. 3
    GGoogle✨ featureFrom Nov 25, 2026You use this

    Guided Vision in Gemini Live comes to iPhone: point your camera and Gemini describes what it sees and tells you how to aim

    What it means for you: If you're on an iPhone and are blind, have low vision or just can't read the small print, you can now share your camera in Gemini Live and get spoken descriptions plus cues like 'tilt down' or 'step back' until it can see what you asked about, such as a nutrition label, a washing-machine dial or a dropped earbud. It came to Android on Oct 1; Google says it can make mistakes and isn't a mobility aid or a replacement for a white cane.

🧰 Job of the day New on the site

Plan gifts for everyone on your list and hit every order-by date

Tell your bot who's on your list, what each person is into and what you want to spend. It suggests a few ideas per person, keeps one tracker of what's picked, ordered, arrived and wrapped, and nudges you each week before the shipping cut-offs, so nobody gets missed and nothing arrives after the holiday.

🛒 Shopping & groceriesEasyWorks in: Grok BotTasks in GeminiChatGPT dots +1

You end up with: One list with ideas for everyone, a running total, and a weekly 'still to buy' note, instead of ideas scattered across screenshots, notes and open tabs, and a gift that turns up on December 27.

Copy today's prompt 
Help me plan holiday gifts. Here's my list: [name, age, what they're into, budget each, e.g. Mum, 67, gardening and crime novels, $60]. My total is [total budget] and I live in [country].

For each person suggest 3 ideas (one useful, one fun, one experience) with a link and a rough price, and the latest date to order so it arrives by [date]. Add a few small extras for [teachers / neighbours / hosts].

Keep one tracker with: person, idea, link, price, ordered, arrived, wrapped, given, and a running total against my budget. Every [Saturday at 10 a.m.] until [last shopping date], send me who still needs a gift, what's late and anything over budget. Remind me 3 days before each order-by date.

Don't buy anything, don't sign in to any store, and don't share anyone's details. I'll order myself from the links and tell you when something is bought.

Watch out: Order-by dates and prices from a bot are a guide. Check the shop's own delivery cut-off and price before you order (BAARP; the todo.is prompt says the same).
