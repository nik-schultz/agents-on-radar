    MMeta🤖 agent launchFrom Nov 24, 2026You use this

    Meta's Muse personal agent launches in the UK, with M&S, ASOS, Currys, easyJet and Jet2 built in

    What it means for you: If you're 18 or over in the UK, you can now use Muse free on iOS, Android, muse.ai or inside WhatsApp to do jobs like Christmas shopping, finding a cheaper energy deal or turning saved travel Reels into an itinerary; it asks before it sends an email or buys anything, and paid plans add more usage. The UK competition regulator (CMA) says consumer law applies to agents like Muse and that Meta made its terms fairer after talks, so read what you agree to before connecting your email or shops.

    MMeta✨ featureFrom Nov 24, 2026You use this

    Meta's Muse agent arrives in Instagram DMs and can turn your saved posts into plans

    What it means for you: If you use Instagram in the US, Canada, Mexico, Japan or Singapore, you can now DM Muse to ask about a post, place or product, catch up on chats you missed, or have it turn saved reels into a trip itinerary, a grocery list from a recipe video, or tidy collections. Meta says nothing is shared, posted or sent without your OK and you can ask Muse to forget or undo things; the save-to-Muse prompt is US and Canada only for now.

  3. 3
    AnAnthropic✨ featureFrom Nov 24, 2026You use this

    Claude can turn an artifact it builds for you into a real Mac or Windows app

    What it means for you: If you use Claude for Mac or Windows, you can ask it to build a small tool (say, a tracker or a meeting-prep page), click "Install as app" in the Share menu, and it opens in its own window with its own icon, can use your connectors and, if you allow it, send notifications or sit in your menu bar. It's a beta on all plans and uses your normal Claude usage; Claude asks for permission again after every update, only apps you (or your company) made can get full computer access, and Anthropic doesn't review apps, so only install ones you trust.

    Source: Claude · Also: Claude release notes, 9to5Mac · 59.5K likes on X

🧰 Job of the day New on the site

Let your bot project-manage your holiday dinner

Hosting Thanksgiving (or any big family meal)? Your bot interviews you, then builds one plan: the menu, who's bringing what, an oven schedule that works back from dinner time, a shopping list and a short text for each guest. On the day it can ping you when it's time to start each dish.

🏡 Family & homeEasyWorks in: Tasks in GeminiGrok BotMeta Muse +2

You end up with: One holiday plan everyone can follow: the menu, who brings what, a cooking timeline that fits your oven, a ready shopping list and reminders on the day, without the planning living only in your head.

Copy today's prompt 
Act as my [Thanksgiving / holiday dinner] project manager. Interview me one question at a time: how many guests (and kids), allergies or diets, what dishes people have already claimed, what time we want to eat, and what my kitchen has ([one oven, four burners, a smoker, a slow cooker]).

Then give me, for my review:
1. The menu and a 'who's bringing what' list. Flag any gaps and suggest who could cover them.
2. A cooking timeline that works back from [4 p.m. Thursday] and fits my one oven, including when to move the turkey to the fridge to thaw.
3. A shopping list sorted by store section.
4. A two-line text for each guest with their dish and arrival time.

Keep what you share short and in plain words. Don't send any texts, place any orders or buy anything without my OK. On the day, remind me when it's time to start each dish. If I tell you something changed, give me a new timeline and show only the lines that changed.

Watch out: Most kitchens have one oven and too many dishes that want it. Tell the bot exactly what you have, or the timeline won't work.
