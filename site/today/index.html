3 things that changed

  1. 1
    xAxAI🏷 pricingFrom Nov 26, 2026You use this

    Grok Bot plans now go about 11% further, and scheduled routines up to 50% further, at the same price

    What it means for you: If you pay for Grok Bot, the same plan now covers about 11% more work, and routines (jobs you set to run on a schedule or when something happens) can use up to 50% less of your allowance, because simple, repetitive steps no longer need a call to the AI model. Prices didn't change and the 50% is the best case, not every routine; Grok Bot's separate hourly run limits still apply, so a routine that runs every hour is now cheaper but not unlimited.

    Source: Sam Sokolin (Grok Bot team) on X · Also: Tesla North, Lapaas Voice · 2.9K likes on X

    MMeta🏢 industryFrom Nov 26, 2026You use this

    Meta turned down the UK financial regulator's offer to test Muse's payments together before the UK launch

    What it means for you: If you use Muse in the UK to shop or renew things for you, no regulator has tested how it pays on your behalf: the Financial Conduct Authority's head of innovation told WIRED that Meta declined a joint sandbox test the week before Tuesday's launch, and the FCA, the competition watchdog and the data watchdog say they're watching the rollout closely. Meta says it has worked constructively with regulators; until there's more scrutiny, keep Muse's purchase approvals on and check every order before you confirm it.

    Source: WIRED · Also: The Next Web, Meta (Muse UK launch) · 23 likes on X

  3. 3
    OAOpenAI🏷 pricingFrom Nov 26, 2026You use this

    OpenAI fixes a GPT-6.2 billing mistake, so paid ChatGPT and Codex users hit limits up to 40% sooner; everyone gets one banked reset

    What it means for you: If you pay for ChatGPT (Go, Plus, Pro or Business) and use GPT-6.2 Astra or Sol, the same work now uses more of your allowance: OpenAI says you'll reach your limits 40% faster on Astra and 25% faster on Sol than last week, because a launch bug had been undercounting them; other models aren't affected and past usage won't be charged. As an apology every Go, Plus, Pro and Business account gets one banked reset, which you redeem in Usage settings on the web or desktop app (not mobile) once an allowance is below 80%, so save it for a day you're about to run out.

🧰 Job of the day New on the site

Get a leave-time check before a long holiday drive

Before a long drive (like the trip home after Thanksgiving), your bot can compare departure times against typical holiday traffic, the weather along the way and official road reports, then check again the morning you leave and tell you: go, shift, or wait. It plans and reminds; you still check the live road report and drive.

✈️ Bookings & travelEasyWorks in: Grok BotTasks in GeminiChatGPT +1

You end up with: A one-page drive plan with a best and backup departure time, the stretches to watch, planned stops, and a morning-of message telling you whether to leave on time.

Copy today's prompt 
I'm driving from [starting city] to [destination city] and need to arrive by [time / 'no deadline']. I could leave [days and time windows]. Riders: [adults, kids, pets]. Car: [gas / hybrid / EV].

1. Suggest a best and a backup departure window and a leave-by time. Use typical holiday traffic patterns and say where your info comes from.
2. List the weather you expect for the hours I'll be on each part of the route, and any mountain passes or stretches I should check on my state's 511 or DOT site. Don't guess at current closures or chain rules; tell me where to check.
3. Plan a stop about every 2 hours ([food / fuel / EV charging / dog walk]).
4. Give me a one-page plan I can share with my passengers.
5. On [departure day] at [7 AM], check the forecast and road reports again and message me: go, shift (to when), or wait, plus anything you couldn't confirm.

Don't book, buy or send anything for me.

Watch out: A chatbot isn't a road report. Scheduled answers can be prepared in advance, and AI tools may not know live closures or chain requirements; check 511 or your state DOT before you leave (Willamette News, citing ODOT and Google).
