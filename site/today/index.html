3 things that changed

  1. 1
    xAxAI✨ featureYou use this

    Grok Bot now works with QuickBooks: ask about cash flow and who owes you, and it drafts invoices and reminders for you to approve

    What it means for you: If you run a small business on QuickBooks Online in the US, you can connect it to Grok Bot and ask about profit, cash flow or overdue customers, then have it draft invoices, estimates and payment reminders that wait for your OK. It's your real books, so set the QuickBooks tools to Needs approval in Grok Bot's per-tool permissions and check every draft before it goes out.

    Source: Grok Bot on X · Also: TeslaNorth, Grok Bot changelog · 1.6K likes on X

    ⚠ Touches Grok Bot: check your settings →
  2. 2
    TcTencent🧠 model releaseYou use this

    Tencent releases the downloadable weights for Hy4, its flagship model, so companies can run it on their own servers

    What it means for you: For most people nothing changes: Hy4 is a 1.5-terabyte download that needs data-centre hardware, so you'd use it through a host such as Together AI rather than on your own computer. Businesses that want to keep data in-house can now run it themselves under Tencent's own licence (not Apache 2.0 like the preview), which needs a separate deal only for companies selling hosted access with over $50 million a year in revenue, or for products with over 100 million monthly users.

    Source: Sulat · Also: Hugging Face (Tencent Hy4) · 1.6K likes on X

  3. 3
    OAOpenAI✨ featureYou use this

    ChatGPT Space, for shared pages and slides that can keep themselves up to date, comes to Plus users

    What it means for you: If you pay for ChatGPT Plus, a new Space tab replaces Library: ask ChatGPT to build a page or slides, like a to-do list or trip plan, set it to update itself from your connected apps, and invite others to edit with you. It's for adults 18+, rolling out gradually; people on the free plan can't open what you share, and there are no public links.

    Source: OpenAI Help Center · Also: Getting started with Space, Engadget · 1.1K likes on X

🧰 Job of the day New on the site

Tell your bot where you put things, then just ask it later

Every time you store, lend or move something, tell your bot in one line. Months later, ask 'where are the spare batteries?' or 'what's in bin 4?' and it answers with the room, shelf or box.

🏡 Family & homeEasyWorks in: ChatGPTClaudeGrok Bot +1

You end up with: A searchable list of what you own and where it is, kept up to date by one-line messages instead of a spreadsheet.

Copy today's prompt 
Be my 'where is it' memory. Keep one list of my things with: item, where it is (room > shelf/box), who has it if lent, and the date of the last change.

When I message a move like '[item] to [place]' or '[item] lent to [name]', update the list and reply with one line confirming it.
When I ask 'where is [item]?' or 'what's in [box]?', answer from the list only. If it isn't on the list, say so; don't guess.
If I send a photo of a box, list what you see and ask me before adding it.
Every [month], send me anything marked 'lent' for more than [30] days.
Never delete an item without asking me.

Watch out: It only knows what you tell it. If someone else moves things and doesn't say, the answer will be out of date. Share the list with your household.
