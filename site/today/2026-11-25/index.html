What changed in AI assistants, what it means for you, and one job to try. About 3 minutes, every weekday.

3 things that changed

  1. 1
    OAOpenAI🏷 pricingYou use this

    From December, ChatGPT and Codex share one usage allowance on Plus, Pro and Business as Chat and Work merge

    What it means for you: If you pay for ChatGPT Plus, Pro or Business, starting in December your chats, agent work and Codex coding will all draw from one allowance measured in tokens, so long agent or coding jobs can use up what you'd otherwise spend on chats; quick chats with the Instant model stay unlimited and don't count. OpenAI says most people won't wait for resets more often than now, but a spokesperson told TechCrunch a small share of the heaviest users may hit limits sooner; you'll see the combined total and reset times in Settings, then Usage, and a small group of Pro users already has the merged Chat and Work screen.

    Source: The Verge · Also: OpenAI Help Center (usage limits), TechCrunch · 7.7K likes on X

    💸 What this means for your bill →
  2. 2
    GGoogle✨ featureYou use this

    Guided Vision in Gemini Live comes to iPhone: point your camera and Gemini describes what it sees and tells you how to aim

    What it means for you: If you're on an iPhone and are blind, have low vision or just can't read the small print, you can now share your camera in Gemini Live and get spoken descriptions plus cues like 'tilt down' or 'step back' until it can see what you asked about, such as a nutrition label, a washing-machine dial or a dropped earbud. It came to Android on Oct 1; Google says it can make mistakes and isn't a mobility aid or a replacement for a white cane.

    Source: @GeminiApp on X · Also: Google blog (Android launch, Oct 1) · 897 likes on X

  3. 3
    OAOpenAI✨ featureYou use this

    OpenAI starts testing Private Inference, which keeps business prompts sealed off even while the model works on them

    What it means for you: Nothing changes in your personal ChatGPT; this is for businesses: OpenAI is testing Private Inference (an alpha) with a small group of customers, running prompts and replies on sealed-off 'confidential computing' hardware with controls customers can verify, similar to how Apple handles Siri's cloud requests. Sam Altman announced the test on Nov 25; OpenAI gave no date for a wider rollout.

    Source: The Next Web · Also: OpenAI Private Intelligence · 8.8K likes on X

🧰 Job of the day New on the site

Let your bot project-manage your holiday dinner

Hosting Thanksgiving (or any big family meal)? Your bot interviews you, then builds one plan: the menu, who's bringing what, an oven schedule that works back from dinner time, a shopping list and a short text for each guest. On the day it can ping you when it's time to start each dish.

🏡 Family & homeEasyWorks in: Tasks in GeminiGrok BotMeta Muse +2

You end up with: One holiday plan everyone can follow: the menu, who brings what, a cooking timeline that fits your oven, a ready shopping list and reminders on the day, without the planning living only in your head.

Copy today's prompt 
Act as my [Thanksgiving / holiday dinner] project manager. Interview me one question at a time: how many guests (and kids), allergies or diets, what dishes people have already claimed, what time we want to eat, and what my kitchen has ([one oven, four burners, a smoker, a slow cooker]).

Then give me, for my review:
1. The menu and a 'who's bringing what' list. Flag any gaps and suggest who could cover them.
2. A cooking timeline that works back from [4 p.m. Thursday] and fits my one oven, including when to move the turkey to the fridge to thaw.
3. A shopping list sorted by store section.
4. A two-line text for each guest with their dish and arrival time.

Keep what you share short and in plain words. Don't send any texts, place any orders or buy anything without my OK. On the day, remind me when it's time to start each dish. If I tell you something changed, give me a new timeline and show only the lines that changed.

Watch out: Most kitchens have one oven and too many dishes that want it. Tell the bot exactly what you have, or the timeline won't work.
