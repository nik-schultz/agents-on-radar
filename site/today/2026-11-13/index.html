What changed in AI assistants, what it means for you, and one job to try. About 3 minutes, every weekday.

Which AI apps do you use? (we'll flag what affects you; saved in this browser only)

3 things that changed

  1. 1
    OAOpenAI🤖 agent launchYou use this

    ChatGPT Wallet lets your ChatGPT agent (a "dot") buy things for you after you approve each purchase

    What it means for you: You add a card once, and when your dot is ready to check out you see the items, total and card, then approve with Face ID or a fingerprint; the store gets a stand-in card number, not your real one, and you keep your card's rewards and dispute protections. It's rolling out only to US ChatGPT Pro subscribers (18+) with US cards for now, and some shops may still block or decline agent purchases.

    Source: OpenAI · Also: TechCrunch · 5.2K likes on X

    ⚠ Touches ChatGPT dots: check your settings →
  2. 2
    SiSierra🏢 industryYou use this

    A new standard called Poppy lets personal AI agents like Meta's Muse deal directly with stores, banks and airlines

    What it means for you: Poppy 1.0 gives your agent an official way in to a company's site, so it can see what it's allowed to do instead of clicking around like a person: it's now on for every Muse user, every Shopify store publishes it automatically, and Mercury, Turkish Airlines and Synchrony are live. You sign in with the company to control what your agent can do, and it must ask you before certain actions like paying.

    Source: Sierra blog · 1.4K likes on X

    ⚠ Touches Meta Muse: check your settings →
  3. 3
    AnAnthropic🤖 agent launchYou use this

    Anthropic invites everyone on the US Claude Duet waitlist, its always-on personal agent

    What it means for you: If you joined the waitlist on a US Pro, Max or Ultra plan, you should now have an invite to Claude Duet, which checks your email and calendar, sends a morning brief and asks before it sends, pays, signs or deletes anything. Its background checks count against your plan's limits, so Anthropic is adding free limit resets during the preview (three on Pro, one on Max and Ultra) that expire December 15.

    Source: Claude Help Center · Also: Frontier Now · 2.3K likes on X

    ⚠ Touches Claude Duet: check your settings →

🧰 Job of the day New on the site

Back up your AI agent so you never lose its memory (and move it to a new computer)

If your agent runs on your own computer or server, its memory, chats, automations and logins live in a folder that one crash could wipe. OpenClaw has a built-in backup and restore, and one user just moved 9 agents and 800+ sessions to a bigger server with 11 minutes of downtime.

💼 Work & careerMediumWorks in: OpenClawHermes Agent

You end up with: A tested backup file of your agent you can restore any time, or the same agent running on a new machine with its memory and automations intact.

Copy today's prompt 
Help me back up my [OpenClaw] agent safely. Walk me through it one step at a time in plain words: stop it, make a verified backup file, and tell me where the file is. Then help me test restoring it into an empty folder so I know the backup works. Don't delete or overwrite anything from my current setup, and don't print any API keys or passwords on screen.

Watch out: The backup holds API keys, logins and chat history. Store it encrypted or somewhere private, never in a public folder.
