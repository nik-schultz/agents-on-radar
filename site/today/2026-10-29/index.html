🧰 Job of the day New on the site

Add an AI to your friends' group chat to make plans happen

Instead of 60 messages about where and when, you add an AI agent to the iMessage group. It suggests options, gets everyone to agree, and then books the table, tickets or rental car for the group once someone says yes.

✈️ Bookings & travelEasyWorks in: InstinctMeta Muse

You end up with: A plan everyone agreed to, already booked, with the details pinned in the same chat where you talked about it.

Copy today's prompt 
You're helping our group plan [a weekend trip to the lake / dinner on Friday]. Ask everyone in the chat for the dates and budget that work, suggest 2 or 3 options with prices, and keep a running list of who's in. Don't book or pay for anything until [I / the organiser] replies "book it", and post the confirmation number in the chat when it's done.

Watch out: The group's agent only sees what's in the chat, not anyone's accounts, but anything you type in the thread is visible to it. Keep private details out of the group (per Instinct's founder, Oct 27, 2026).
