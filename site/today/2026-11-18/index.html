  1. 1
    GGoogle🧠 model releaseYou use this

    Google releases Gemini 4 Neon, a faster Gemini 4 model it pitches as close to its top Carbon model on hard work

    What it means for you: If you pay for Google AI Pro or Ultra, you can now pick Gemini 4 Neon ("Deeper analysis") in the Gemini app for long documents, spreadsheets and multi-step agent jobs, and Google says it makes things up less often than its earlier models.     Source: Google · Also: Gemini API pricing, 9to5Google · 18.9K likes on X

    xAxAI✨ featureYou use this

    Grok Bot can now search and book flights, hotels and rental cars for you in one chat

    What it means for you: Tell your Grok Bot where and when you're going and it compares fares, rooms and cars, showing bag, change and refund rules, maps and reviews, and books the option you pick. Some airlines, such as United, don't let AI agents finish a booking, and when the Bot can't confirm a final purchase it sends you the link instead, so check the details before you tap Book.

    Source: Grok Bot on X · Also: Grok Bot changelog · 3.8K likes on X

    Canadians can now plan meals, fill a grocery basket and check out with Loblaw stores inside Meta's Muse

🧰 Job of the day New on the site

Get a morning check-in from your fitness watch that says how you slept and what to train today

Connect your Garmin (or Oura, Strava or Withings) data to an AI assistant and it reads your sleep, heart-rate variability (HRV), recent runs and training load, then gives you a short morning note: how recovered you look, how last week went, and a suggested session for today. Some connectors can also put an approved workout straight onto your watch.

🩺 Health & careMediumWorks in: ClaudeChatGPTGrok +1

You end up with: A two-minute morning read on your recovery and training, plus an approved workout waiting on your watch if you want one.

Copy today's prompt 
You're my training check-in. Use only my [Garmin] data from the connector. If something is missing, say "no data" instead of guessing.

1. Last night: sleep time, HRV and resting heart rate, each compared with my own last [14] days.
2. This week so far: sessions done, total time, and how hard they were (time in each heart-rate zone).
3. Say in one line whether I look well recovered, normal, or tired, and which numbers made you say that.
4. Suggest one session for today that fits my goal of [a 10K on 15 March / getting fitter / losing 5 kg], plus an easier option if I feel worse than the numbers say.
5. If I ask you to put a workout on my watch, show me the exact session and date first and wait for my yes.

Keep it under 150 words. Don't change or delete anything in my account, and remind me this isn't medical advice if anything looks unusual.

Watch out: This is coaching guidance, not medical advice. If you feel unwell, have an injury or a health condition, or your numbers suddenly look very different, talk to a doctor or a qualified coach.
