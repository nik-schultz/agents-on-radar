3 things that changed

  1. 1
    xAxAI✨ featureYou use this

    Grok Bot can now phone businesses for you, like cancelling a plan or booking an appointment

    What it means for you: Tell Grok Bot who to call and why, approve the call, and it waits on hold, works the phone menu and talks to the agent while you watch a live transcript and can take over at any time, then it sends you a summary. It only calls U.S. numbers for now (not Washington or Illinois), says up front that it's a virtual assistant, and some reps may refuse to deal with an AI.

    Source: TeslaNorth · Also: Teslarati · 15.8K likes on X

    ⚠ Touches Grok Bot: check your settings →
  2. 2
    OAOpenAI✨ featureYou use this

    ChatGPT Images 2.6 can make animated GIFs and remember a saved reference photo

    What it means for you: Ask ChatGPT for a looping GIF or to animate a still photo, and save a photo (say, of your dog) under a name so you can reuse it later by typing @ and that name. It's on all ChatGPT plans and in Codex, about 50% faster than before, with GIFs rolling out gradually; ChatGPT can't edit an animated GIF you upload.

    Source: OpenAI Help Center · Also: heise online, OpenAI system card · 22K likes on X

  3. 3
    OAOpenAI🏢 industryYou use this

    OpenAI's AI agents found and drafted patches for 948 Linux 'get root' security holes

    What it means for you: In one mid-October test, OpenAI's autonomous security pipeline checked the Linux kernel, proved 948 ways a normal user could become admin, and wrote candidate patches for human review, at about $1.2 million in API cost. Humans still review and report the fixes; the run used an upcoming cyber model that became GPT-6 Cyber.

    Source: OpenAI · 12.8K likes on X

🧰 Job of the day New on the site

Keep a visa application on track with an AI agent

Tell an agent "get my visa by the end of November" and it lists what you need, tracks missing documents and reminds you until it's done. It can also spot an upcoming trip in your calendar and flag visa rules and arrival forms.

✈️ Bookings & travelEasyWorks in: Meta MuseChatGPT dotsGrok Bot

You end up with: One running checklist for your visa and entry forms, with reminders, so nothing is missed before you fly.

Copy today's prompt 
I'm travelling to [country] on [dates] with a [nationality] passport for [tourism/business]. Look up the entry and visa rules ONLY on official government websites and link them. Make me a checklist of documents, fees, appointments and deadlines, including any digital arrival card. Every [Monday], remind me what's still missing and what's due next. Don't fill out or submit any form or pay anything without asking me first.

Watch out: Agents can be wrong or out of date: The Straits Times found Muse suggested a restaurant that had already closed. Always confirm visa rules on the official government site.
