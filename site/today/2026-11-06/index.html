3 things that changed

  1. 1
    MMeta✨ featureYou use this

    Meta brings Muse Wonder-style memory and focus to Muse and Meta AI

    What it means for you: Muse and Meta AI get specialized models trained with Muse Wonder so they remember what still matters across chats (Active Memory) and keep pushing when a task hits a snag (Active Focus). Meta says Muse forgets critical goal details 2.4x less often and finishes everyday tasks with surprises 67% more often; Meta AI should ask sharper follow-ups and handle mid-chat course changes better.

    Source: Meta AI Research · Also: AI at Meta on X · 5.5K likes on X

    ⚠ Touches Meta Muse: check your settings →
  2. 2
    MMeta✨ featureYou use this

    Meta's Muse can now build you a custom 'space' like a planner, tracker or game

    What it means for you: Describe what you want (a gift tracker, wedding planner, school-events dashboard or trip planner) and Muse builds it as a space inside the app that you can pin to your tabs and keep editing. If you connect apps like Gmail, Calendar or OpenTable, a space updates itself in the background; it's available everywhere Muse is.

    Source: Meta Newsroom · Also: TechCrunch, The Verge · 2.5K likes on X

    ⚠ Touches Meta Muse: check your settings →
  3. 3
    AnAnthropic🏢 industryYou use this

    Anthropic explains how 'dreaming' helps Claude fix and organize its memory between sessions

    What it means for you: Anthropic shows how Claude can go back over its own past work while idle to correct wrong notes, merge duplicates and build a wiki of what it knows, so a long-running agent repeats fewer mistakes. It's an engineering explainer, not a new button, but it's a good model for any bot you run: have it review and tidy its notes regularly.

    Source: Anthropic · 14.4K likes on X

🧰 Job of the day New on the site

Have an AI agent research your family history for you

MyHeritage just launched GAIA, an AI research agent that takes a question like 'who were my great-grandfather's parents?', searches old records and your family tree on its own, and shows you what it found with a confidence rating. Nothing goes into your tree until you approve it.

🏡 Family & homeEasyWorks in: MyHeritage GAIA

You end up with: A research session on one ancestor with the records found, how sure the agent is about each one, and suggested next places to look, which you then check and choose to save.

Copy today's prompt 
Research [full name], born around [year] in [place]. I already know: [spouse, children, parents, any dates]. I'm stuck on [what you want to find, e.g. his parents or where he emigrated from]. Show me the records you find, how confident you are in each, and what conflicts with my tree. Don't add anything to my tree without asking me.

Watch out: MyHeritage says AI answers can contain errors. Open the actual records and check them before you add anything to your tree.
