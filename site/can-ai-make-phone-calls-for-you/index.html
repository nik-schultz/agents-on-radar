Phone calls, in plain English

Can AI make phone calls for you?

Short answer: yes. Some personal AI agents can now phone a restaurant, a vet or a salon, book the slot, sit through a customer-service call and tell you what was said. You give the job in a message; the bot makes the call from its own number. It works best for simple bookings and questions, and you should keep payments and account changes for yourself.

Updated Nov 10, 2026 · By BotsDoThat

What an AI can do on the phone for you

  • Book things: give your bot a phone number so it can call and book for you, and book restaurant tables by message, even at phone-only places. One user's bot booked his dog's vet appointment by phone (X post); another's books restaurant tables with strict rules (X post).
  • Customer-service calls: have your bot make a customer-service phone call for you, for example cancelling a subscription (X post).
  • Answer your calls: let a bot answer your phone and take messages for you.
  • Travel trouble: get roadside help abroad when nobody speaks your language.

Which AI can make phone calls for you?

  • Grok Bot: once you connect a phone-calling add-on so the bot has its own number. The calls above were made this way.
  • Meta Muse: can call businesses for you; see booking tables by agent for a test where it booked easily but failed to cancel.
  • Instinct: messages or calls small businesses; an invite-only beta when WIRED reviewed it (WIRED).
  • Cue by Manus: each agent gets its own phone number and can make and take calls (Manus blog: Introducing Manus 2.0 and Cue).

Want to build your own? The Phone Call Bot blueprint has the instructions to paste in, the guardrails and the cost of a call.

What AI phone calls cost

Calls are billed by the minute, not by the token. Prices on the official pricing pages of three voice-agent platforms:

  • Retell AI (Pay-as-you-go): $0.07–$0.31 a minute; Retell's voice engine ($0.055/min) plus the AI model and voice you pick. A 5-minute call: $0.35–$1.55. (official pricing, checked Nov 10, 2026)
  • Bland (Start): $0.14 a minute; the AI model, speech-to-text and voices, with a $0 platform fee. A 5-minute call: $0.70. (official pricing, checked Nov 10, 2026)
  • Vapi (Pay as you go): $0.05 a minute; Vapi's hosting fee only; speech-to-text, the AI model and the voice are charged on top at the providers' cost. A 5-minute call: $0.25. (official pricing, checked Nov 10, 2026)

What goes wrong

  • Some companies won't deal with a bot. They only act for the account holder; one airline wouldn't rebook a flight for that reason (X post).
  • People hang up on spam. Have the bot say it's an AI right away (X post).
  • A call is hard to undo. Tell it exactly what it may agree to, and never let it share card numbers, deposit details or one-time codes.
  • Check it worked. Look for the confirmation email or make sure the next charge doesn't appear.

Questions people ask

Can AI make phone calls for you?

Yes. Some personal AI agents can now phone a business for you, book a table or an appointment, make a customer-service call and tell you what was said. Grok Bot can do it once you connect a phone-calling add-on that gives it its own number, Meta Muse can call businesses for you, and Cue by Manus gives each agent its own phone number. Make it say it's an AI, and keep anything involving money or your account details for yourself.

Which AI can make phone calls for me?

Grok Bot with a phone-calling add-on connected, Meta Muse, Instinct (an invite-only beta, per WIRED in September 2026) and Cue by Manus. Developers can also build a call bot on a voice-agent platform such as Retell AI, Vapi or Bland.

How much does it cost for AI to make a phone call?

On the voice-agent platforms we checked (Nov 10, 2026), $0.05 to $0.31 a minute (the lowest, Vapi's $0.05, is a hosting fee before the AI model and voice), so a 5-minute call is about $0.25 to $1.55 before any extras (table with sources). Agent apps may instead include calls in a plan or charge for the phone number separately.

Can an AI call customer service and wait on hold for me?

Yes, with limits. One user shared a real Hulu cancellation call made by Grok Bot with a phone-calling add-on (X post). But some companies only act for the account holder: another user says an airline wouldn't rebook his flight because he wasn't on the line (X post).

Will the person on the phone know it's an AI?

Have it say so at the start. One user says a restaurant owner almost hung up, thinking it was spam, until the bot said it was an AI (X post).

Is it safe to let an AI make calls for me?

For simple bookings and questions, yes, if you set rules: say what it may agree to, never let it share card numbers or one-time codes, and check afterwards that the change really happened. If calls are recorded, check the recording rules where you live; some places need everyone's consent.
