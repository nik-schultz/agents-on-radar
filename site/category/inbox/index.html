Blueprints for this

  • ⏱ Every weekday at 7:00am

    Morning Inbox Briefing

    Wake up to a 2-minute summary of what's in your email and what needs you today.

  • ⏱ Every morning at 8:00am (or every few hours for time-sensitive pages)

    Web Page Watcher

    Get one ping when a page you care about changes (a restock, a new listing, an appointment slot, a status update) and stop refreshing it yourself.

  • ⏱ Only when you ask it to (never on its own)

    Phone Call Bot

    Hand off the phone calls you keep putting off: booking a table, a vet visit or a haircut, or a quick customer-service call.
