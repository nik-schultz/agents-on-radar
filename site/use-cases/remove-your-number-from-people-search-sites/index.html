Get your phone number off people-search sites to cut spam calls

A bot finds where your phone number is listed on people-search and data-broker sites, files the opt-out requests, and checks again later.

MediumAdded Oct 20, 2026♥ 6 · ↻ 0 on X · measured Oct 22, 2026

What the bot does

  1. Searches people-search and data-broker sites for your name and phone number.
  2. Submits each site's opt-out form using only the details it needs.
  3. Clicks the confirmation links those sites email you.
  4. Re-checks on a schedule you choose, because listings can come back.

What you end up with

A list of where your number was listed, opt-outs filed, and a regular re-check.

Copy-paste prompt

Fill in the [brackets]. Then paste it into any tool below.

Copy this prompt 
Find where my name and phone number [name, city, phone] are listed on people-search and data-broker sites. Make me a list with links. Then submit each site's official opt-out request using only the minimum details it asks for, and confirm any verification emails. Don't pay for any removal service or create accounts without asking me. Re-check every [month] and tell me what's new.

Watch out

  • Some opt-out pages ask for more personal info than they had. Share only the minimum.
  • Listings can reappear, so the re-check matters.
  • This cuts spam calls that come from your number being sold, but won't stop every robocall.
