Get 5 weekend activity ideas that fit your kids and the weather

Every Friday, get activity ideas matched to your kids' ages, the weather and your budget, with a rainy-day backup.

EasyUpdated Oct 20, 2026 · By BotsDoThat · Added Oct 20, 2026

What the bot does

  1. Checks the weekend forecast for your area.
  2. Finds activities that fit your kids' ages and interests.
  3. Adds costs and whether booking is needed.
  4. Includes a wet-weather alternative.

What you end up with

A Friday list of 5 doable weekend plans.

Copy-paste prompt

Fill in the [brackets]. Then paste it into any tool below.

Copy this prompt 
Every Friday, suggest 5 family activities for this weekend based on our children's ages [ages], interests [interests], location [town], the weather and a budget of [$]. Include estimated costs, booking requirements and a wet-weather alternative.
