Run open models on your own computer (or a cheap host) for simple jobs

Open-weight models can run on your own computer with a free tool like Ollama, so routine jobs cost nothing per message. Some hosts also run open models for a fraction of big-model prices.

AdvancedAdded Oct 21, 2026Popularity: not measured

What the bot does

  1. Installs a local model runner like Ollama on your computer.
  2. Downloads an open model that fits your machine.
  3. Points your bot's simple, private jobs (sorting, summarizing notes) at the local model.
  4. Keeps a paid cloud model only for jobs the local one can't handle.

What you end up with

Routine bot jobs that run on your own hardware with no per-message bill, and your private text staying on your machine.

Plans and limits change; check each tool's page.

Watch out

  • Local models are usually less capable than top cloud models. Test on real examples first.
  • Your computer has to be on, and big models need a lot of memory.
  • Licences vary. Mistral says its open-weight models are Apache 2.0 for research and individual use, while commercial deployments need a Mistral licence.
