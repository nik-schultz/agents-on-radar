Keep a household essentials list and reorder on time

A bot keeps a running list of essentials, asks once a week what's low and builds the reorder.

EasyAdded Oct 20, 2026Popularity: not measured

What the bot does

  1. Keeps a list of your usual essentials.
  2. Once a week, asks what's running low.
  3. Builds a reorder list or cart.
  4. Reminds you about anything you forgot last time.

What you end up with

No more running out of toilet paper or lunchbox snacks.

Copy-paste prompt

Fill in the [brackets]. Then paste it into any tool below.

Copy this prompt 
Maintain a list of our household essentials: [list]. Every Saturday morning, ask me what we're running low on. Build a cart with those items at [store] and stop before checkout. Remember what we bought and when, and remind me if something usually runs out by now.
