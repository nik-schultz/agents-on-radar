Build a list of local leads and approve every email

A workflow finds businesses in your niche and area, researches each one and drafts a personal email. Nothing goes out until you approve it.

AdvancedAdded Oct 20, 2026♥ 72 · ↻ 4 on X · measured Oct 23, 2026

What the bot does

  1. Finds businesses by type and location.
  2. Identifies the decision maker and a public contact.
  3. Researches each business and drafts a personal note.
  4. Saves everything to a spreadsheet for you to review.
  5. Sends only approved emails, slowly.

What you end up with

A spreadsheet of researched leads with drafted, approved outreach.

Copy-paste prompt

Fill in the [brackets]. Then paste it into any tool below.

Copy this prompt 
Find [20] [type of business] in [city] that might need [your service]. For each: name, website, decision maker if public, one specific detail about them, and a 3-sentence personal email draft. Put it all in a sheet. Do not send anything.
