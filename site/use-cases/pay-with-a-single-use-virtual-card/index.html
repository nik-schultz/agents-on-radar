Give your agent a single-use card before it buys anything

When an agent books or buys for you, give it a one-time virtual card so it can't be charged again.

EasyUpdated Oct 28, 2026 · By BotsDoThat · Added Oct 20, 2026♥ 9 · ↻ 0 on X · measured Oct 30, 2026

Seen on X ×2 · YouTube · Official docs

What the bot does

  1. You create a single-use or merchant-locked virtual card in your bank or card app.
  2. The agent uses that card for one booking.
  3. The card stops working after one use or past its limit.

What you end up with

Agent purchases with a built-in spending limit.

Copy-paste prompt

Fill in the [brackets]. Then paste it into any tool below.

Copy this prompt 
For this booking, use only the virtual card ending in [last 4]. Its limit is [$]. If the total is higher, stop and ask me. Never save this card to the site.

Tools that can do it today

  • agentChatGPT Work (cloud browser)Paid ChatGPT plans only (not Free or Go); rolling out
  • agentInstinctInvite-only beta (per WIRED, Sep 2026)
  • agentGrok Bot (personal agents)Included with paid individual Cursor plans and Cursor Teams; or link SuperGrok, SuperGrok Plus or SuperGrok Heavy; or a self-serve Grok Business seat
  • agentMeta MuseFree with a usage limit; paid tier for more

Plans and limits change; check each tool's page.

Watch out

  • Check whether your bank offers single-use or merchant-locked cards before you start.
  • A prepaid agent balance (like Link balance in Instinct) caps each purchase, not your total, and Link's terms make you responsible for the agent's mistakes. Keep the balance small (Link's setup link takes $150 or less at a time).

What this costs to run

In a chat app it runs inside your plan. If you run it as a bot on an AI API, the bill depends on how often it runs and how much it reads.
