Set up a “chief of staff” bot that plans your other bots

Instead of building lots of bots, start with one that asks what you do and suggests which helpers you need.

EasyUpdated Oct 20, 2026 · By BotsDoThat · Added Oct 20, 2026♥ 104 · ↻ 6 on X · measured Oct 23, 2026

Seen on X · YouTube ×3

What the bot does

  1. Lists what a human chief of staff would handle for you.
  2. Writes its own job description (the “system prompt”).
  3. Suggests the first 1–3 helper bots and what each one does.
  4. Briefs those helpers so you don't have to.

What you end up with

A short, sensible plan for which bots you need, often fewer than you thought.

Copy-paste prompt

Fill in the [brackets]. Then paste it into any tool below.

Copy this prompt 
You're my Chief of Staff bot. Give me a list of all the things a typical chief of staff would be responsible for, then suggest a system prompt you can install for yourself.

About me: [who you are and what you do].
What I want help with: [goals].
Suggest the first bots you'd hire and what each one owns.
