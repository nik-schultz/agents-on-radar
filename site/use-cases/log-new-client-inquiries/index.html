Log every new client inquiry automatically

When an inquiry email arrives, the bot pulls out the client's name and requested date, logs it in your tracker and sets up a folder.

EasyUpdated Oct 20, 2026 · By BotsDoThat · Added Oct 20, 2026

What the bot does

  1. Watches for inquiry emails (“are you available on…”).
  2. Pulls out the name, date and what they want.
  3. Adds a row to your client tracker sheet.
  4. Creates a folder named after the client.

What you end up with

A client tracker that fills itself in, so no inquiry slips.

Copy-paste prompt

Fill in the [brackets]. Then paste it into any tool below.

Copy this prompt 
When I receive an email asking about my [service], extract the client's name, requested date and request, and log it in my 'Client Tracker' sheet. Then create a new folder named after the client. Draft a friendly reply with my availability, but don't send it.
