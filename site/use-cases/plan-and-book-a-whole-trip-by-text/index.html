Plan and book a whole trip by texting your bot

One parent says his bot booked the flight, the rental car, the vacation rental, a restaurant and event tickets, all over text.

MediumUpdated Oct 20, 2026 · By BotsDoThat · Added Oct 20, 2026♥ 62 · ↻ 12 on X · measured Oct 23, 2026

Seen on X

What the bot does

  1. Takes your dates, budget and who's coming.
  2. Finds and compares flights, cars and places to stay.
  3. Books each piece after you say yes.
  4. Makes restaurant bookings and buys event tickets.
  5. Keeps every confirmation in one thread.

What you end up with

A booked trip with every confirmation in one chat.

Copy-paste prompt

Fill in the [brackets]. Then paste it into any tool below.

Copy this prompt 
Plan a trip for [who] to [place] from [date] to [date], budget [$]. I need: a flight from [city] (no red-eyes), a car, a place to stay near [area] with [needs], one dinner reservation on [night] and tickets to [event]. Show me 2 options for each with prices. Book only after I reply YES for that item, and send me every confirmation number in one list.

Watch out

  • Use a card with a low limit, or a single-use virtual card.
  • Double-check dates, names and cancellation rules before you say yes.

What this costs to run

In a chat app it runs inside your plan. If you run it as a bot on an AI API, the bill depends on how often it runs and how much it reads.
