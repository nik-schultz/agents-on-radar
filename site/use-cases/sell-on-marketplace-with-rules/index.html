Sell on Marketplace with an agent, with safety rules set first

Agents can answer buyers and schedule pickups, but set rules first. One story tells of an agent sharing an address and booking a pickup when the seller was out.

MediumAdded Oct 20, 2026♥ 10 · ↻ 0 on X · measured Oct 22, 2026

What the bot does

  1. Answers buyer questions using your listing details.
  2. Proposes pickup times from your calendar.
  3. Asks you before sharing any address or agreeing a price.
  4. Keeps a log of every buyer conversation.

What you end up with

Buyer messages handled, with you in charge of price, address and timing.

Copy-paste prompt

Fill in the [brackets]. Then paste it into any tool below.

Copy this prompt 
Help me sell [item] on [Marketplace]. You may answer questions about condition and size using this description: [details]. You may NOT: share my address, agree a price under [$], or schedule a pickup, without asking me first. Only offer pickup times from my calendar where I'm marked 'home'.
