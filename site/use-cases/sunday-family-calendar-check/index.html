Catch family calendar clashes before the week starts

Every Sunday a bot reads the next two weeks of your family calendar and school notices and flags the clashes.

EasyUpdated Oct 20, 2026 · By BotsDoThat · Added Oct 20, 2026♥ 506 · ↻ 34 on X · measured Oct 23, 2026

What the bot does

  1. Reads your shared family calendar for the next 14 days.
  2. Reads recent school notices for dates you haven't added yet.
  3. Spots clashes between work, school and activities.
  4. Lists anything you need to prepare, like forms, payments or kit.

What you end up with

A short Sunday note: what's coming up, what clashes, and what to prepare.

Copy-paste prompt

Fill in the [brackets]. Then paste it into any tool below.

Copy this prompt 
Every Sunday at 6pm, review our family calendar for the next 14 days. Highlight important events, scheduling conflicts, missing information and anything we need to prepare. Keep it under 10 bullet points. Don't change or delete any calendar events. Just tell me.
