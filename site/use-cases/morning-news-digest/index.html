Get a short morning digest on topics you care about

A bot reads the news on your topics every morning and sends you a short plain-English digest, or even an audio version.

EasyUpdated Oct 20, 2026 · By BotsDoThat · Added Oct 20, 2026

Seen on X · Reddit · YouTube ×2 · Official docs

What the bot does

  1. Checks news on your chosen topics every morning.
  2. Picks the few stories that matter.
  3. Explains each in plain English.
  4. Sends it as a message, a document or audio.

What you end up with

A short daily briefing instead of doom-scrolling.

Copy-paste prompt

Fill in the [brackets]. Then paste it into any tool below.

Copy this prompt 
Every morning at 7am, send me the 5 most important stories about [topics] from the last 24 hours, in plain English, 2 sentences each, with a link to the original source. Skip anything that's just opinion.
