Write a resignation letter that keeps the door open

You're leaving a job and want to say so clearly and kindly, without oversharing. AI can draft a short, professional resignation letter or email in a couple of minutes.

EasyAdded Oct 20, 2026Popularity: not measured

What the bot does

  1. Tell it your last working day, who it goes to, and the tone you want (warm, neutral or brief).
  2. Say what you do and don't want to mention, like the new job or the reason you're leaving.
  3. Let it draft a letter and a shorter email version.
  4. Check the notice period in your contract or employee handbook and fix the dates if needed.
  5. Read it once out loud, make it sound like you, and send it after you've told your manager in person or on a call.

What you end up with

A short, polite resignation letter or email with your last day, a thank-you and a handover offer, ready to send to your manager.

Copy-paste prompt

Fill in the [brackets]. Then paste it into any tool below.

Copy this prompt 
Write a resignation letter for me.

My role: [JOB TITLE]
Manager's name: [NAME]
Today's date: [DATE]
My last working day: [DATE]
Tone: [WARM / NEUTRAL / VERY BRIEF]
Things to mention: [e.g. thanks for the mentoring on X, happy to train my replacement]
Things NOT to mention: [e.g. where I'm going, why I'm leaving]

Keep it under 150 words, positive and professional. No complaints and no reasons unless I gave one. Then give me a 3-sentence email version. Don't send anything; I'll review it.

Watch out

  • Check your contract or handbook for the required notice period before you pick a last day.
  • Keep it short and positive. A resignation letter usually ends up in your personnel file.
  • Don't paste confidential company details into the chat. The AI only needs your role, dates and tone.

Questions people ask

Can AI write my resignation letter?

Yes. Give it your last day, your manager's name and the tone you want, and it will draft a short letter. Read it once and change anything that doesn't sound like you before you send it.

Do I have to give a reason for resigning?

Usually not. A resignation letter only needs to say you're resigning and give your last day. A thank-you and a handover offer are a kind extra.
