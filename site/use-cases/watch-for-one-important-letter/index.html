Watch your email for one important letter or reply

Waiting on a visa decision, a parcel or a reply from the landlord? Have a bot watch for it and ping you.

EasyUpdated Oct 20, 2026 · By BotsDoThat · Added Oct 20, 2026

Seen on Reddit

What the bot does

  1. Takes who or what you're waiting for.
  2. Checks your inbox daily.
  3. Pings you when it arrives, with a summary.
  4. Tells you if a deadline is attached.

What you end up with

A ping the moment it arrives, so you stop checking.

Copy-paste prompt

Fill in the [brackets]. Then paste it into any tool below.

Copy this prompt 
Check my email daily for a message from [sender/organization] about [topic]. When it arrives, message me a 3-line summary and any deadline or action it asks for. Stop checking once it arrives.

Tools that can do it today

  • agentGrok Bot (personal agents)Included with paid individual Cursor plans and Cursor Teams; or link SuperGrok, SuperGrok Plus or SuperGrok Heavy; or a self-serve Grok Business seat
  • agentGemini SparkGoogle AI Pro and Ultra subscribers in select countries
  • automationZapierA simple email filter rule plus a text alertFree plan; paid plans add more

Plans and limits change; check each tool's page.

Watch out

  • Look in spam yourself too; some bots can't see it.

What this costs to run

In a chat app it runs inside your plan. If you run it as a bot on an AI API, the bill depends on how often it runs and how much it reads.
