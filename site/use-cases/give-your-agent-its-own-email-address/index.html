Give your agent its own email address

Give your agent an inbox of its own. Then you or anyone else can forward it jobs, like a meeting request it turns into a calendar invite with prep notes.

MediumUpdated Oct 20, 2026 · By BotsDoThat · Added Oct 20, 2026♥ 17 · ↻ 1 on X · measured Oct 23, 2026

What the bot does

  1. Gets its own email address.
  2. Reads whatever arrives there.
  3. Turns requests into actions, like adding a calendar invite.
  4. Researches the sender and adds prep notes.

What you end up with

A forwarding address for chores. Forward it a request and it gets handled.

Copy-paste prompt

Fill in the [brackets]. Then paste it into any tool below.

Copy this prompt 
When an email arrives at your address: if it's a meeting request, find a 30-minute slot in my calendar next week, create a draft invite and add 3 bullet points of research about the sender. If it's anything else, summarize it and ask me what to do. Never reply to the sender without my OK.

Watch out

What this costs to run

In a chat app it runs inside your plan. If you run it as a bot on an AI API, the bill depends on how often it runs and how much it reads.
