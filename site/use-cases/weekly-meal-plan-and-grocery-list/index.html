Make a weekly meal plan and grocery list

Deciding what's for dinner every night is exhausting, and you keep buying food that goes to waste.

EasyUpdated Oct 19, 2026 · By BotsDoThat · Added Oct 19, 2026

Seen on YouTube ×2

What the bot does

  1. List what's in your fridge and pantry.
  2. Fill in the prompt and swap any meals you don't like.
  3. Copy the grocery list to your phone or a grocery app.
  4. Save the prompt and reuse it every week.

What you end up with

Seven dinners your household will actually eat, using what you already have, plus one grocery list sorted by store aisle.

Copy-paste prompt

Fill in the [brackets]. Then paste it into any tool below.

Copy this prompt 
Plan [NUMBER] dinners for [NUMBER] people this week.
Likes: [FOODS]. Dislikes/allergies: [LIST]. Weeknight cooking time: [MINUTES].
Already in my fridge/pantry: [LIST].
Budget: [AMOUNT OR 'KEEP IT CHEAP'].

1. Give me the plan, reusing ingredients across meals so nothing goes to waste.
2. Make one grocery list grouped by store section, minus what I already have.
3. Note which meals make good leftovers for lunch.
