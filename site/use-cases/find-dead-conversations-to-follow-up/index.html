Find the conversations you forgot to follow up on

A weekly automation reads your calendar, inbox and tasks and lists people still waiting to hear back, with drafted nudges.

MediumUpdated Oct 20, 2026 · By BotsDoThat · Added Oct 20, 2026♥ 5 · ↻ 2 on X · measured Oct 26, 2026

Seen on X

What the bot does

  1. Looks at recent meetings, email threads and tasks.
  2. Finds threads where you owe a reply or the other person went quiet.
  3. Drafts a short, friendly follow-up for each one.
  4. Puts it all in one morning brief.

What you end up with

A list of dropped threads with a ready-to-send nudge for each.

Copy-paste prompt

Fill in the [brackets]. Then paste it into any tool below.

Copy this prompt 
Every Monday at 8am, look at my sent email, my calendar from the last 30 days and my task list. List conversations where (a) I promised something and haven't sent it, or (b) I asked something and got no reply in 7+ days. For each, draft a 2-sentence friendly follow-up. Don't send anything.
