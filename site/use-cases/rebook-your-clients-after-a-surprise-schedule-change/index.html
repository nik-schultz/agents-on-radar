Rebook all your clients after a surprise schedule change

When a surgery, a sick day or a family emergency wipes out your week, your bot moves everyone for you. It looks at when each client usually comes, offers each one a new slot that fits, updates the calendar when they say yes, and sends reminders the night before.

Seen on X ×2 · Reddit

What the bot does

  1. Lists every appointment in the days you lost, from your calendar or booking app.
  2. Looks at each client's past visits to learn the day and time they usually come.
  3. Writes each client a short, friendly message offering one or two new slots that fit their usual pattern, and shows you the messages before they go out.
  4. Updates the calendar only after a client confirms, and keeps a list of who hasn't answered.
  5. Every evening, reminds the next day's clients of their time and details.

What you end up with

A rebuilt schedule with every client offered a slot that suits them, in about an hour instead of a full day of texting, plus automatic reminders.

Copy-paste prompt

Fill in the [brackets]. Then paste it into any tool below.

Copy this prompt 
My schedule changed: I can't work from [first date] to [last date]. Help me move everyone.

1. List every client appointment in those dates from my [Google Calendar / booking app], with name, service and length.
2. For each client, look at their past appointments and tell me the day and time they usually come.
3. Find open slots from [new start date] onward that fit each client's usual day and time. Don't double-book, and keep [my break / closing time] free.
4. Draft one short, friendly message per client: '[My business] here. I have to move your [service] on [old date]. Would [slot 1] or [slot 2] work?' Show me all drafts before sending anything.
5. When I approve, send them on [WhatsApp / text / email]. Only change the calendar after a client says yes. Keep a list of who hasn't answered after [24] hours.
6. From now on, every evening at [6 pm], remind tomorrow's clients of their time and address.

Never share one client's details with another, and ask me before offering a discount or anything I didn't list.

Setup

  1. Connect your calendar or booking app and the app you message clients with (WhatsApp, text or email). Check that past appointments have the client's name in them.
  2. Run steps 1 and 2 of the prompt first and read the list. If it's missing people, fix the calendar before going on.
  3. Approve the message drafts in a batch, but read a few in full first, especially for regulars.
  4. Let it send, then check back in an hour. Clients who ask for something different go to you.
  5. Keep the evening reminders running after the rush. They cut no-shows.

Watch out

  • Bulk messages from a new number or account can be flagged as spam. Send from the number your clients already know.
  • Ask it to change the calendar only after each 'yes'. Otherwise you can end up with two clients in one slot.
  • Client names, phone numbers and appointment history are private. Connect only the calendar that holds them, and keep health details out of messages.
  • The salon story is one owner's report shared by her husband (X, 2 likes); test it on a few clients before trusting it with all of them.
