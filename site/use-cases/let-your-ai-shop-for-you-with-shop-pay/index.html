🛒 Shopping & groceries

Let your AI find and buy things for you with Shop Pay

Connect Shopify's free Shop skill to ChatGPT or Claude (or use Shop Pay inside Muse, Instinct, Claude Duet or Grok Bot) and your AI can search online stores, fill in a checkout and pay with Shop Pay. You approve every purchase, and your card number is never handed to the AI.

EasyUpdated Oct 29, 2026 · By BotsDoThat · Added Oct 29, 2026♥ 85 · ↻ 7 on X · measured Oct 29, 2026

Seen on X · Official docs

What the bot does

  1. You describe what you want in plain words, like "waterproof hiking boots under $150, size 10, delivered by Friday".
  2. It searches products from Shopify stores, using your Shop sizes, preferences and past orders if you've signed in.
  3. It shows you a few picks with prices, shipping and the store's return policy.
  4. When you pick one, it prepares the checkout with your saved address already filled in.
  5. You review the total and approve the payment in Shop. Afterwards it can track the order and check if it's returnable.

What you end up with

The thing you wanted, found and ordered from one chat, with every purchase approved by you and the order tracked in your Shop app.

Copy-paste prompt

Fill in the [brackets]. Then paste it into any tool below.

Copy this prompt 
Use the Shop connection to help me buy [what you want, e.g. a rain jacket for a 10-year-old]. My budget is [$] including shipping, and it needs to arrive by [date]. Only show items from stores that accept returns for at least 30 days. Give me your top 3 with the total price, delivery date and return window for each. Don't check out or pay for anything until I reply "buy number [1/2/3]".

Setup

  1. If you don't have one yet, make a free Shop account at shop.com (it's the same Shop app many stores use for package tracking).
  2. Go to shop.md and choose Add to ChatGPT or Add to Claude. For another AI, copy the prompt shop.md gives you and paste it into a chat.
  3. When your AI asks you to sign in to Shop, open the link and approve it on Shop's own page. Never type your Shop code or passkey into the chat.
  4. Already using Muse, Instinct, Claude Duet or Grok Bot? Just ask it to buy something and follow its prompt to connect Shop Pay.
  5. Leave "Allow agent to pay for me" off at first, so you approve every order. You can turn it on later in the Shop app under Account > Connections, with a weekly, monthly or total budget.

Watch out

  • Shop's help center says agents can get prices, taxes, shipping, stock or return details wrong. Check the total and the store before you approve.
  • When the agent buys for you it accepts the store's shipping, return and cancellation rules on your behalf. Tell it to skip stores that don't take returns.
  • If you approved a purchase or gave the agent a budget, disputing the charge later may not work (Shop help center). Start with approval for every order.
  • Connecting shares your name, email, phone, addresses and order history with the AI app. You can disconnect any time in Shop, but that doesn't delete what was already shared.

What this costs to run

In a chat app it runs inside your plan. If you run it as a bot on an AI API, the bill depends on how often it runs and how much it reads.
