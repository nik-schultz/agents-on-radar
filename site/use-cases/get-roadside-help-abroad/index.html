Get roadside help abroad when nobody speaks your language

Broken down in another country and the insurer's phone line is in a language you don't speak? An agent can find the right help form, fill it in with your details and get help moving.

EasyUpdated Oct 21, 2026 · By BotsDoThat · Added Oct 21, 2026♥ 0 · ↻ 0 on X · measured Oct 23, 2026

What the bot does

  1. Takes where you are, the car, and what happened.
  2. Finds your insurer's or the rental company's roadside-assistance form or number for that country.
  3. Fills in the form with your details and location.
  4. Tells you what it submitted and what happens next.

What you end up with

A roadside-assistance request filed in the local system, and a plain summary of what comes next.

Copy-paste prompt

Fill in the [brackets]. Then paste it into any tool below.

Copy this prompt 
My car broke down at [location / share live location] in [country]. Car: [make, model, plate]. Insurer or rental company: [name, policy or booking number]. Find their official roadside-assistance form or number for this country. Fill in the form with my details, translate anything I need to know, and show me what you'll submit before you send it.

Watch out

  • If you're in danger, call the local emergency number first (112 across the EU).
  • Check the insurer's official site so the bot doesn't use a look-alike form.

What this costs to run

In a chat app it runs inside your plan. If you run it as a bot on an AI API, the bill depends on how often it runs and how much it reads.
