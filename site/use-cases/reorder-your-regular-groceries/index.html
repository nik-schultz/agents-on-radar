Let an agent reorder your regular groceries

Some people now let an agent handle their weekly grocery order and switch stores when a better offer appears.

MediumAdded Oct 20, 2026♥ 96 · ↻ 4 on X · measured Oct 21, 2026

What the bot does

  1. Knows your usual items and brands.
  2. Builds the weekly order on a grocery site that allows agents.
  3. Swaps in a better offer when it finds one.
  4. Asks for your OK (“confirm”) before paying.

What you end up with

Groceries ordered after a quick chat and one confirm tap.

Copy-paste prompt

Fill in the [brackets]. Then paste it into any tool below.

Copy this prompt 
Here's my usual weekly grocery list: [list with brands and sizes]. Build this order on [Instacart / store site]. If an item is out of stock, pick the closest match and tell me. Stop at checkout and show me the total.

Watch out

  • Some sites block agents. Amazon's Conditions of Use now include rules for AI agents, and since mid-October Grok Bot users (and Meta Muse users in September) report being blocked there. Don't ask a bot to get around it: use another store or finish the Amazon order yourself.
  • Always review substitutions and the final total before paying.
