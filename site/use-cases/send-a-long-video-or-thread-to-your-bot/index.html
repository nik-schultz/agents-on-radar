Send a long video or thread to your bot with one reply

See a long video or X thread you don't have time for? Reply to it by tagging your bot, and a short summary waits for you in your bot's app later.

EasyAdded Oct 21, 2026♥ 65 · ↻ 6 on X · measured Oct 22, 2026

What the bot does

  1. Watches for posts where you tag it with a request like "summarize this and send it to my bot".
  2. Reads the thread or video you replied to.
  3. Writes a short summary in plain words.
  4. Drops the summary into your bot's chat so you can read it when you have a minute.

What you end up with

A read-later pile of short summaries, saved without copying links or leaving the app you were scrolling.

Copy-paste prompt

Fill in the [brackets]. Then paste it into any tool below.

Copy this prompt 
When I send you a video or thread to save, write me a summary with: one sentence on what it is, the 3 to 5 main points, anything I should act on, and the link. Keep it under 150 words. Put new summaries in one running list, newest first, and once a day tell me how many are waiting. Don't reply to or post anything on X for me.

New to this? Here's how to set up a bot in 5 plain steps →

Setup

  1. Connect your bot to your X account first, so it knows which tags come from you.
  2. Try it on one short post before trusting it with long videos.

Watch out

  • Your reply that tags the bot is public, so anyone can see what you saved.
  • Summaries can miss nuance. Open the original before you quote it or act on it.
