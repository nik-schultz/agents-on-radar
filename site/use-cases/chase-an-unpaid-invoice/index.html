Chase an unpaid invoice without burning the relationship

A client is late paying and you're not sure how firm to be.

EasyAdded Oct 19, 2026Popularity: not measured

What the bot does

  1. Gather the invoice, agreed terms and any emails about the work.
  2. Run the prompt and send the first reminder.
  3. Put the follow-up dates on your calendar.
  4. Update your invoice template for future clients.

What you end up with

A sequence of three reminders (friendly, firm, final) and a clean invoice template for next time.

Copy-paste prompt

Fill in the [brackets]. Then paste it into any tool below.

Copy this prompt 
A client owes me [AMOUNT] for [WORK], invoice [NUMBER] dated [DATE], due [DATE]. Our relationship: [GOOD / NEW / TENSE].
Payment terms we agreed: [TERMS, IF ANY].

1. Write 3 short emails: a friendly reminder, a firm follow-up a week later, and a final notice that states next steps.
2. Keep them professional and factual; include invoice number, amount and how to pay.
3. Suggest clearer payment terms for my future invoices.
4. Give me a simple invoice template with the fields I should always include.

Watch out

  • Only state next steps (late fees, collections) that your agreement and local law allow.
  • Keep client names and bank details out of the chat; add them yourself.

Questions people ask

Can AI make the invoice too?

Yes, it can draft an invoice layout. Many accounting and payment apps also generate invoices; use whichever your clients can pay most easily.

Which AI tools can do this?

ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok, Grok Bot (personal agents) or Zapier can do this today. Paste the copy-paste prompt on this page into any of them and fill in the [brackets].
