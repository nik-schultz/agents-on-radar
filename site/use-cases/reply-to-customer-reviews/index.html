Reply to customer reviews (even the angry ones)

Reviews pile up on Google and you don't have time to answer, or a harsh review has you stuck on what to say.

EasyUpdated Oct 19, 2026 · By BotsDoThat · Added Oct 19, 2026

Seen on YouTube

What the bot does

  1. Copy new reviews from your Google Business Profile (or other site).
  2. Run the prompt and edit each reply so it sounds like you.
  3. Post replies from your verified Business Profile.
  4. Do it weekly so it never piles up.

What you end up with

Short, personal replies for each review, including a calm, professional reply to a negative one.

Copy-paste prompt

Fill in the [brackets]. Then paste it into any tool below.

Copy this prompt 
I run [BUSINESS TYPE] called [NAME]. Write replies to these reviews:
[PASTE REVIEWS]

Rules:
- Under 60 words each, warm and specific to what the customer said.
- For negative reviews: thank them, acknowledge the issue without arguing, say what we're doing about it, and invite them to contact [EMAIL/PHONE] offline.
- No discounts or incentives, no customer private details.
- Vary the wording so replies don't look copy-pasted.

Watch out

  • Google checks replies against its content policies before posting. Keep replies polite and free of private information.
  • Don't let AI invent facts about what happened.

What this costs to run

In a chat app it runs inside your plan. If you run it as a bot on an AI API, the bill depends on how often it runs and how much it reads.

Questions people ask

Do I need anything to reply on Google?

Google says your business must be verified before you can reply to reviews. Replies show as a response from the business, and the reviewer gets notified.

Which AI tools can do this?

ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok or Zapier can do this today. Paste the copy-paste prompt on this page into any of them and fill in the [brackets].

How hard is it to set up?

It's rated Easy. Start with the steps under “What the bot does”.

How much time can it save?

Sprinklr reports that Jackson Hewitt cut its average Google review handling time from 10 minutes to around 2 after centralizing reviews in Sprinklr with its AI+ features and a library of pre-approved replies. This is a vendor case study. Source: Sprinklr customer story: Jackson Hewitt.

What should I watch out for?

Google checks replies against its content policies before posting. Keep replies polite and free of private information.
