Hand your agent a quick errand from your smartwatch

Meta's Muse agent now has an Apple Watch and Wear OS app. Say or type a quick errand on your wrist, like finding movie tickets or fixing a calendar clash, and approve the result without pulling out your phone.

EasyUpdated Nov 10, 2026 · By BotsDoThat · Added Nov 10, 2026♥ 4,949 · ↻ 677 on X · measured Nov 12, 2026

Seen on X

What the bot does

  1. Takes your request from the watch by voice call, dictation or a typed message.
  2. Does the work in the cloud with the apps and sites you connected (calendar, email, booking sites), even after you close the app.
  3. Comes back with a suggestion, for example 'your dentist appointment clashes with a meeting, move it to 4pm?'.
  4. Waits for you to tap approve before it books, sends or buys anything.

What you end up with

Small errands started from your wrist and finished in the background, with you approving the final step.

[Say this to your agent from the watch] Find two tickets for [movie] near [area] this [day] after [time]. Check my calendar for clashes first. Show me the best two options and wait for me to approve before booking or paying.

Setup

  1. Set up the agent on your phone first and connect only the accounts you want it to use (for example calendar and email).
  2. Install the watch app: on Apple Watch it comes with the Muse iPhone app (watchOS 9 or later); on Wear OS get it from Google Play.
  3. Add the watch-face shortcut (Apple Watch complication) so it's one tap away.
  4. Keep approvals on for anything that sends, shares or buys, and check the activity log on your phone the first few times.

Tools that can do it today

  • agentMeta MuseFree app; Apple Watch needs watchOS 9+ and the iPhone app; Wear OS also supportedFree with a usage limit; paid tier for more
  • chatGeminiBuilt into many Wear OS watches for reminders and quick questionsFree plan; paid plans add more

Plans and limits change; check each tool's page.

Watch out

  • The watch is only a remote: the agent works in the cloud with whatever accounts you connected, so connect as few as you need (Gadgets & Wearables).
  • Meta's own Google Play listing warns Muse may give inaccurate information or take unexpected actions, so read the summary before you approve.
  • A small screen makes it easy to tap 'approve' without reading. Save bigger bookings or payments for your phone.
  • Meta hasn't published a full list of compatible Wear OS watches, so check your model first (Times of India Gadgets Now).

What this costs to run

In a chat app it runs inside your plan. If you run it as a bot on an AI API, the bill depends on how often it runs and how much it reads.
