Get a researched report with sources on any question

Ask one big question and let a research agent spend several minutes searching, reading and comparing pages. You get back a written report with links to every source.

EasyUpdated Oct 24, 2026 · By BotsDoThat · Added Oct 24, 2026

Seen on Hacker News ×2 · YouTube · Official docs

What the bot does

  1. Takes your question and asks a few follow-ups if it's unclear.
  2. Plans a set of searches, then reads dozens of pages.
  3. Compares what the sources say and notes where they disagree.
  4. Writes a report with a link next to each claim.

What you end up with

A multi-page report with linked sources that you can check, instead of 30 open tabs.

Copy-paste prompt

Fill in the [brackets]. Then paste it into any tool below.

Copy this prompt 
Research this for me: [your question, e.g. which home battery backup options work with a [brand] solar system in [state]]. Who it's for: [me / my family / my small business]. I care most about [safety, cost, reliability]. Give me a short summary first, then the details. Link a source for every claim, say when sources disagree, and tell me what you couldn't confirm. Don't buy, sign up or contact anyone.

Setup

  1. Open ChatGPT, Gemini or Claude and pick the research mode (ChatGPT: Deep research in the tools menu; Gemini: Deep Research from the menu in the text box; Claude: + then Research, with web search on).
  2. Type your question with who it's for and what matters most.
  3. Answer any follow-up questions it asks, then let it run. It can take several minutes.
  4. Open three or four of the linked sources and check the claims you'll act on.

Tools that can do it today

  • chatChatGPTDeep researchFree plan; paid plans add more
  • chatGeminiDeep Research; free accounts get a limited numberFree plan; paid plans add more
  • chatClaudeResearch, on paid plansFree plan; paid plans add more

Plans and limits change; check each tool's page.

Watch out

  • Research agents can still cite a page that doesn't say what the report claims. Click the links for anything important.
  • Treat the report as a first draft. Check the sources for anything you'll act on, spend money on or repeat to others.
  • Claude's help page says Research can use your connected apps (like Gmail) without asking each time. Turn off connectors you don't want it reading.

What this costs to run

In a chat app it runs inside your plan. If you run it as a bot on an AI API, the bill depends on how often it runs and how much it reads.
