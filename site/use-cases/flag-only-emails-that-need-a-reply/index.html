Only see the emails that actually need a reply

A simple triage bot flags anything needing a reply within 24 hours and moves the noise out of the way.

EasyUpdated Oct 20, 2026 · By BotsDoThat · Added Oct 20, 2026Popularity: not measured

What the bot does

  1. Reads new email as it arrives.
  2. Flags messages that need a reply from you within a day.
  3. Labels newsletters, receipts and promos.
  4. Leaves everything else alone.

What you end up with

An inbox where the flagged emails are the only ones you need to answer.

Copy-paste prompt

Fill in the [brackets]. Then paste it into any tool below.

Copy this prompt 
Go through my new email every hour. If a message needs a reply from me within 24 hours, star it and add a one-line reason. Label newsletters 'Read later', receipts 'Receipts' and promotions 'Promos', and archive them. Never delete or reply.
