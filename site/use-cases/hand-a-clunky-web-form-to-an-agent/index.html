Hand a clunky web form or app to an agent that uses the computer for you

Some software you only touch once a year. A computer-use agent can click through it for you, from a login you control, while you watch.

EasyAdded Oct 21, 2026♥ 3 · ↻ 0 on X · measured Oct 23, 2026

What the bot does

  1. Opens the site or app in its own browser.
  2. Asks you to sign in yourself (or uses a password manager you set up).
  3. Fills in the form from the details you gave it.
  4. Stops before the final submit and shows you what it entered.

What you end up with

The form filled in correctly without you learning the software, ready for your final click.

Copy-paste prompt

Fill in the [brackets]. Then paste it into any tool below.

Copy this prompt 
Open [website or app] and fill in the [form name] for me. Here are the details: [list everything it needs]. When you reach a login, stop and let me sign in. If any field is unclear, ask me instead of guessing. Stop before the final Submit button and show me a summary of everything you entered.
