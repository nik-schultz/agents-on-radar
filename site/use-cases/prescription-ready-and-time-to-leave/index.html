Get pinged when a prescription is ready, and told when to leave

A personal agent can sweep your email every morning for "ready for pickup" notices, and check live traffic before your appointments to tell you when to head out.

EasyAdded Oct 21, 2026♥ 47 · ↻ 0 on X · measured Oct 23, 2026

What the bot does

  1. Checks your email every morning for pharmacy notices.
  2. Pings you the moment a prescription is ready for pickup.
  3. Looks at your calendar for today's appointments.
  4. Checks live traffic and tells you exactly when to leave.

What you end up with

A ping when your prescription is ready and a "leave now" nudge before each appointment.

Copy-paste prompt

Fill in the [brackets]. Then paste it into any tool below.

Copy this prompt 
Every morning at [8:00am], check my email for messages from [pharmacy name] saying a prescription is ready, and message me right away if you find one. Also look at my calendar for today. For each appointment with an address, check live traffic from [home/work] and tell me what time to leave to arrive [10] minutes early. Don't reply to any emails or change my calendar.

Watch out

  • It only works if your pharmacy sends email or text notices to an account the agent can read.
  • Health details are sensitive. Use an agent you trust with your email.
