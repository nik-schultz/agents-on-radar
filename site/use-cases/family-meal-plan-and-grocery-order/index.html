Plan a week of family meals and fill the grocery cart

A bot keeps your recipe list, writes the week's meal plan and turns it into a shopping list that fills your grocery cart.

MediumUpdated Oct 20, 2026 · By BotsDoThat · Added Oct 20, 2026♥ 1,015 · ↻ 29 on X · measured Oct 27, 2026

Seen on X ×3 · Reddit · YouTube

What the bot does

  1. Keeps a simple list of the recipes your family actually eats.
  2. Every week, picks meals that fit your schedule and budget.
  3. Prints a meal plan you can stick on the fridge.
  4. Turns the plan into one shopping list, grouped by aisle.
  5. Hands the list to a shopping agent that fills your Instacart cart. You check out yourself.

What you end up with

A printed weekly meal plan and a filled grocery cart waiting for your OK.

Copy-paste prompt

Fill in the [brackets]. Then paste it into any tool below.

Copy this prompt 
Every Thursday, plan [5] dinners for [2 adults and 2 kids]. Use only recipes from my recipe list below, avoid [allergies], keep the week under [$ budget]. Reuse ingredients so little goes to waste. Give me: (1) a one-page meal plan I can print, (2) one shopping list grouped by store section, leaving out what I said we already have. Don't buy anything.

My recipes: [paste a list or link]
Already in the fridge: [list]

Plans and limits change; check each tool's page.

Watch out

  • Do the checkout yourself. Read the cart before you pay.
  • Some stores block shopping agents. One user says Amazon blocked his agent, so he moved his groceries to Instacart.
