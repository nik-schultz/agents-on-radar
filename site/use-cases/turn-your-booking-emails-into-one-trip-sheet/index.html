Turn scattered booking emails into one trip sheet that flags clashes

Your flight, hotel, train and dinner confirmations are spread over five emails. Put them in one folder (or forward them) and an AI agent reads them all, writes one day-by-day trip sheet with every time, address and confirmation code, and warns you about clashes, like a flight that lands after the hotel desk closes.

EasyUpdated Nov 16, 2026 · By BotsDoThat · Added Nov 16, 2026♥ 42 · ↻ 1 on X · measured Nov 19, 2026

Seen on X · Official docs

What the bot does

  1. Reads only the booking emails or PDFs you put in one folder, or the ones you forward to it.
  2. Pulls out each booking's date, time, place, confirmation code and any deadline (check-in, cancellation cut-off).
  3. Checks that each date's weekday is right and lists any email that isn't about this trip.
  4. Writes one trip sheet, day by day in time order, that you can open on your phone.
  5. Puts a list of clashes at the top: overlapping bookings, arriving somewhere after it closes, or a checkout after your train leaves.
  6. Shows you the sheet and saves it only after you say yes. It doesn't book, change or cancel anything.

What you end up with

One trip sheet with every booking in time order and a short list of problems to fix before you leave.

Copy-paste prompt

Fill in the [brackets]. Then paste it into any tool below.

Copy this prompt 
Turn my booking confirmations into one trip sheet and tell me what clashes. Look only at [the folder name / the files I attached]. Use only what the confirmations say; where they're silent, write "not in the confirmations".

1. List each file with a few words on what it is, and name any that aren't about my trip to [destination] on [dates].
2. Check every date's weekday and flag any that don't match.
3. Write the trip sheet day by day, in time order: each booking with its time, address and confirmation code, plus anything I must do first (online check-in, ID, deadlines).
4. Put a list of clashes at the top: overlapping bookings, places I'd reach after they close, and tight connections under [60] minutes.
5. Show me the whole sheet and wait for my yes before you save it as [trip-sheet] in that folder.

Don't book, change, cancel or email anyone.

Setup

  1. Make a new folder (or an email label) and put only this trip's confirmation emails and PDFs in it. Most mail apps let you save or print an email as a PDF.
  2. Hermes Agent or Claude Cowork: open a new chat, give it that one folder, paste the prompt and fill in the brackets. ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini in a browser: attach the PDFs or screenshots to the chat instead.
  3. Read the clash list first and fix those with the airline or hotel yourself.
  4. Check each line against the original email once. Then save the sheet to your phone or share it with whoever is travelling with you.
  5. Prefer an app? Travel Wallet, Moonjar and the open-source UB Trippin each give you an email address you forward confirmations to, and they build the trip for you.

Watch out

  • AI can misread a faded PDF, an odd layout or a name spelled two ways. Travel Wallet's own guide says to check each ticket's name, date, time and seat before you go.
  • Give the agent one folder of copies, not your whole inbox or Documents. That keeps it away from unrelated mail and files.
  • Confirmation codes and booking references let someone change your booking. Don't post the sheet publicly or share it beyond the people on the trip.
  • A trip sheet doesn't follow live delays. Keep your airline app's alerts on for schedule changes.
  • Forwarding services read whatever you send them. Only forward the booking emails, not threads with other personal details.

What this costs to run

In a chat app it runs inside your plan. If you run it as a bot on an AI API, the bill depends on how often it runs and how much it reads.
