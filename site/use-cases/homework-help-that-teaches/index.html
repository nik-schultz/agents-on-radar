Use AI for homework help that teaches instead of cheats

Your kid is stuck on homework, you don't remember how to do it, and you don't want the AI to just hand over answers.

EasyAdded Oct 19, 2026Popularity: not measured

What the bot does

  1. Check your child's school rules on AI use first.
  2. Paste the prompt and the problem.
  3. Sit with your child for the first few sessions.
  4. Ask the AI to explain the idea to you too, so you can help next time.

What you end up with

A patient, step-by-step tutor that asks your child questions and explains the idea, without doing the work for them.

Copy-paste prompt

Fill in the [brackets]. Then paste it into any tool below.

Copy this prompt 
Act as a patient tutor for my [GRADE]-grade child. Topic: [SUBJECT/TOPIC].
Here is the problem they're stuck on: [PASTE OR PHOTO]

Rules:
- Do NOT give the final answer.
- Ask one question at a time to help them figure out the next step.
- If they're stuck twice, give a hint or a similar example with different numbers.
- Use simple words and short sentences.
- At the end, give them one practice problem to try on their own.

Questions people ask

Will it just give my kid the answer?

Not if you set the rules in the prompt like the one above. Remind it if it slips.

Which AI tools can do this?

ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or Grok can do this today. Paste the copy-paste prompt on this page into any of them and fill in the [brackets].

It's rated Easy. Time it saves, by our editor's estimate: less time stuck and fewer evening battles. Start with the steps under “What the bot does”.
