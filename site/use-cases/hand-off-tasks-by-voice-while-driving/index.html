Hand off tasks to your agent by voice while you drive

Long drive? Talk to your agent on a voice call and hand off research, follow-ups and to-dos, then find them done when you arrive. Since Nov 11, 2026 Gemini works on Apple CarPlay too, so ChatGPT, Claude, Grok and Gemini can all be reached from the car screen.

EasyUpdated Nov 11, 2026 · By BotsDoThat · Added Oct 21, 2026♥ 1 · ↻ 0 on X · measured Nov 11, 2026

Seen on X · YouTube · Official docs

What the bot does

  1. Takes a hands-free voice call from you.
  2. Turns what you say into tasks: research, drafts, reminders, routine changes.
  3. Works on them while you drive.
  4. Leaves a summary of what's done and what needs your OK.

What you end up with

A list of tasks started or finished during the drive, waiting for your review.

Copy-paste prompt

Fill in the [brackets]. Then paste it into any tool below.

Copy this prompt 
I'm driving, so keep replies short and spoken. I'll list tasks one at a time. For each one, repeat it back in one sentence and start it. Research and drafts are fine; don't send, post, buy or book anything until I review it later. When I hang up, write me a summary: done, in progress, waiting for my OK.

Setup

  1. Update your agent's iPhone or Android app and allow the microphone.
  2. Start one voice chat on your phone first (Gemini requires this before it shows on CarPlay).
  3. Connect only the accounts you want it to read on the road, like calendar and email.
  4. Before you set off, tap the app on the car screen and say what you want handed off; check the results after you park.

Tools that can do it today

  • agentGrok Bot (personal agents)Voice call with an agent, used in the source postIncluded with paid individual Cursor plans and Cursor Teams; or link SuperGrok, SuperGrok Plus or SuperGrok Heavy; or a self-serve Grok Business seat
  • chatChatGPTVoice mode for quick hand-offsFree plan; paid plans add more
  • chatGeminiGemini Live on Android Auto and, from Nov 11, 2026, Apple CarPlay; reads your Gmail and Calendar if connectedFree plan; paid plans add more

Plans and limits change; check each tool's page.

Watch out

  • Use hands-free only, and keep your eyes on the road. Don't read the summaries until you've stopped.
  • Have it hold anything that sends or buys until you can check it properly.
  • Gemini on CarPlay needs the latest Gemini iPhone app, microphone permission and one Gemini Live chat on your phone first. Daily Brief isn't available under 18 or in the EEA (Google).
  • If your agent can read your email, it may read out private details to everyone in the car. Ask for summaries only when you're alone.

What this costs to run

In a chat app it runs inside your plan. If you run it as a bot on an AI API, the bill depends on how often it runs and how much it reads.
