Set up medication and appointment reminders for a parent

Family agents can remind an older parent about medication and book routine appointments, with the family kept in the loop.

EasyAdded Oct 20, 2026Popularity: not measured

What the bot does

  1. Sends medication reminders at set times.
  2. Asks for a quick “done” reply and tells the family if there's no reply.
  3. Books routine appointments, like the dentist, after a family member approves.
  4. Keeps a simple log.

What you end up with

Reminders that actually reach Mum or Dad, and a family that knows they did.

Copy-paste prompt

Fill in the [brackets]. Then paste it into any tool below.

Copy this prompt 
Remind [Mum] at [8am and 8pm] to take her [medication] by message. Ask her to reply 'done'. If she hasn't replied within 1 hour, message me. Once a month, ask me whether any appointments need booking. Never change medication instructions and never give medical advice.
