Turn the flood of school emails into one weekly digest

Label school emails and let an agent read them weekly. It pulls out deadlines, forms, payments and events, without duplicates.

MediumUpdated Oct 26, 2026 · By BotsDoThat · Added Oct 20, 2026

Seen on X · YouTube · Official docs

What the bot does

  1. Reads emails with your “School” label, including linked newsletters.
  2. Pulls out deadlines, permission slips, payments and events.
  3. Ignores grades and activities that aren't your kids'.
  4. Removes duplicates and puts urgent things first.
  5. Can add events to your calendar and to-dos to your list.

What you end up with

One digest a week with nothing important buried in a newsletter link.

Copy-paste prompt

Fill in the [brackets]. Then paste it into any tool below.

Copy this prompt 
Every Sunday at 6am, read all emails labeled 'School' that aren't labeled 'Processed'. Open any newsletter links. Extract permission slips, payments, deadlines, events and anything needing a reply. My kids: [initials, grades, schools]. Ignore other grades and [sports]. Put district-wide closures at the top, remove duplicates, and label each email 'Processed' when done. Draft replies for my approval; don't send.
