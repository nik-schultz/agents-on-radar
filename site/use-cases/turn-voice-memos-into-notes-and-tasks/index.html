Turn rambling voice memos into tidy notes and a to-do list

Talk into your phone while walking or driving, then let AI turn the transcript into organized notes, a to-do list and any drafts you mentioned.

EasyUpdated Oct 24, 2026 · By BotsDoThat · Added Oct 24, 2026

Seen on Hacker News · YouTube ×2 · GitHub

What the bot does

  1. Takes the transcript of your voice memo.
  2. Groups your thoughts by topic and removes the “ums” and repeats.
  3. Pulls out to-dos, dates and people to contact.
  4. Drafts any message or note you said you needed to write.

What you end up with

Clean notes and a task list from ideas you'd otherwise forget.

Copy-paste prompt

Fill in the [brackets]. Then paste it into any tool below.

Copy this prompt 
Here's a transcript of a voice memo I recorded: [paste]. Turn it into: 1) a short summary, 2) notes grouped by topic, 3) a to-do list with any dates I mentioned, 4) drafts of any messages I said I'd send. Keep my wording where you can. Mark anything you're guessing with (?).

Setup

  1. Record a memo in your phone's recorder app. Many recorder apps now show a transcript; otherwise upload the audio to an AI app that accepts audio files.
  2. Copy the transcript.
  3. Paste it with the prompt into ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini.
  4. Move the to-dos into your task app or calendar.

Tools that can do it today

  • chatChatGPTFree plan; paid plans add more
  • chatClaudeFree plan; paid plans add more
  • chatGeminiFree plan; paid plans add more

Plans and limits change; check each tool's page.

Watch out

  • Transcripts mishear names and numbers. Check phone numbers, amounts and dates before acting on them.
  • Don't record other people without their permission.
  • Delete memos with private details once you've saved the notes.

What this costs to run

In a chat app it runs inside your plan. If you run it as a bot on an AI API, the bill depends on how often it runs and how much it reads.
