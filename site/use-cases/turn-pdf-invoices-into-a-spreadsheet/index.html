Turn a pile of PDF invoices and receipts into a spreadsheet

Drop in a stack of invoices or receipts and get one tidy table: date, vendor, amount and tax for each, ready for your bookkeeper or a spreadsheet.

MediumUpdated Oct 24, 2026 · By BotsDoThat · Added Oct 24, 2026

Seen on Reddit · Hacker News ×2 · YouTube

What the bot does

  1. Reads each PDF or photo, even when layouts differ.
  2. Pulls out the fields you choose, like date, vendor, invoice number, amount and tax.
  3. Puts everything in one table and flags anything it couldn't read.
  4. Can run automatically for new invoices that arrive by email.

What you end up with

One spreadsheet of every invoice, instead of typing them in by hand.

Copy-paste prompt

Fill in the [brackets]. Then paste it into any tool below.

Copy this prompt 
Here are [12] invoices. Make a table with one row per invoice and these columns: date (YYYY-MM-DD), vendor, invoice number, total, tax, currency, file name. If a value isn't on the invoice, write “missing”. Don't calculate or guess values. After the table, list any invoice you had trouble reading.

Setup

  1. Put the invoices in one folder. Start with 10 or fewer.
  2. Upload them to ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini with the prompt.
  3. Spot-check at least three rows against the original PDFs, especially totals.
  4. Download or copy the table into your spreadsheet. To automate new invoices, an n8n workflow can extract the same fields as they arrive.

Watch out

  • Check totals against the originals. AI can misread a 7 as a 1 or pick the wrong total on a busy invoice.
  • Invoices include business and bank details. Use a business account or a tool your accountant approves.
  • Keep the original files. Your tax records need the documents, not just the table.

What this costs to run

In a chat app it runs inside your plan. If you run it as a bot on an AI API, the bill depends on how often it runs and how much it reads.
