Set up an AI coach that helps you (or your teen) start overwhelming tasks and checks in

A parent shared how they set up ChatGPT as a "getting started" coach for their 20-year-old son with ADHD: one chat for schedule and check-ins, a Project for each big job so it remembers progress, and instructions to ask one question at a time and give one five-minute first step. Scheduled check-ins keep it going without anyone nagging.

EasyUpdated Oct 29, 2026 · By BotsDoThat · Added Oct 29, 2026

Seen on Reddit · Official docs

What the bot does

  1. It keeps one main chat for your schedule, goals and daily check-ins, plus a separate Project for each big job (clean the room, register for college) so it remembers where you left off.
  2. When you're stuck, you tell it (or show it a photo or video of the mess) and it asks one question at a time.
  3. It gives you one small next step with a clear end, ideally five minutes or less, and fits the plan around your real schedule.
  4. If you miss a day, it shrinks the next step and restarts without piling on extra work.
  5. A scheduled task can check in at a set time each day ("Did you start the first step?") and send a phone or email notification.

What you end up with

A plan with small steps and a finish date for the job you've been avoiding, and a coach that remembers your progress and nudges you on a schedule.

Copy-paste prompt

Fill in the [brackets]. Then paste it into any tool below.

Copy this prompt 
You're my getting-started coach. I find it hard to start big tasks. Rules: ask me one question at a time; look at my real schedule ([work hours, classes]); give me ONE next step that takes five minutes or less and has a clear end; if I say I'm stuck, make the step smaller; if I miss a day, restart with a small step and no guilt trip. My first job is: [clean and clear out my old bedroom by (date)]. Make a plan with three small stages and breaks, then ask me what I want to do first.

Setup

  1. In ChatGPT, make a Project for the big job (for example "Clean my room") and paste the coach rules above into the Project's instructions.
  2. Start by describing the job, or attach a photo or short video so you don't have to explain the mess in words.
  3. Ask it: "Every day at [6pm], check in on today's step and give me the next one." It creates a scheduled task. Allow notifications when asked (Settings > Notifications).
  4. If you're setting this up for your teen or adult child, do it on their own account with them, and let them choose the check-in time.
  5. After a week, review: ask "What did I finish, and what should I change about the plan?"

Watch out

  • This is a helper for getting started, not ADHD treatment or a replacement for a doctor, therapist or coach.
  • A scheduled task created inside a Project can't read files you uploaded to that Project (OpenAI Help Center). Put key details in the task's instructions.
  • Free ChatGPT allows only 3 active scheduled tasks and runs them at most once a day in a rough time window (morning, afternoon, night). Exact times need a paid plan (OpenAI Help Center).
  • Keep health details, passwords and account numbers out of task titles and instructions; shared task links show them (OpenAI Help Center).
  • The parent who shared this says it's still early. Check after a couple of weeks whether it's actually helping.

What this costs to run

In a chat app it runs inside your plan. If you run it as a bot on an AI API, the bill depends on how often it runs and how much it reads.
