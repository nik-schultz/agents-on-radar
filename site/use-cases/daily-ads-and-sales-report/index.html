Get your ads and sales numbers every morning without logging in

A bot logs into your ad and sales dashboards (read-only) and sends a morning summary with what changed.

MediumUpdated Oct 20, 2026 · By BotsDoThat · Added Oct 20, 2026♥ 4 · ↻ 0 on X · measured Oct 27, 2026

Seen on X ×2

What the bot does

  1. Checks your ad accounts and sales dashboard each morning.
  2. Compares yesterday with the past week.
  3. Highlights big changes and the likely reason.
  4. Sends one short report.

What you end up with

A 1-minute morning report instead of 5 dashboards.

Copy-paste prompt

Fill in the [brackets]. Then paste it into any tool below.

Copy this prompt 
Every morning at 7am, pull yesterday's results from [ad platforms] and [store/sales platform]. Give me: spend, sales, cost per sale, and anything that changed more than 20% from the 7-day average, with a likely reason. Read-only: never change budgets or ads.

Tools that can do it today

  • agentClaude CoworkPaid Claude plans only (Pro, Max, Team, Enterprise)
  • agentGrok Bot (personal agents)Included with paid individual Cursor plans and Cursor Teams; or link SuperGrok, SuperGrok Plus or SuperGrok Heavy; or a self-serve Grok Business seat
  • automationZapierFree plan; paid plans add more
  • automationMakeFree plan; paid plans add more

Plans and limits change; check each tool's page.

Watch out

  • Read-only access first. Don't let it change budgets until you've watched it for weeks.

What this costs to run

In a chat app it runs inside your plan. If you run it as a bot on an AI API, the bill depends on how often it runs and how much it reads.
