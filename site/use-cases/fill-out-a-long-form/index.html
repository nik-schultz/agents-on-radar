Get through a long form without the headache

A long application or government form is confusing, and you're not sure what each question is really asking.

MediumAdded Oct 19, 2026Popularity: not measured

What the bot does

  1. Get the official form and its official instructions.
  2. Use the prompt to understand each question and gather documents.
  3. Enter your answers yourself on the official site or paper form.
  4. Ask the AI to double-check your answers for consistency (without ID numbers).
  5. Keep a copy of what you submitted.

What you end up with

A question-by-question explanation, a list of documents to have ready, and draft answers for you to check and enter yourself.

Copy-paste prompt

Fill in the [brackets]. Then paste it into any tool below.

Copy this prompt 
I need to fill out this form: [FORM NAME AND WHO IT'S FOR]
Here are the questions or a copy of the form (personal info removed): [PASTE OR UPLOAD]

1. Explain what each question is asking in plain English.
2. List every document or number I'll need before I start.
3. Point out questions where a wrong answer could cause problems or delays.
4. Where I give you my details, draft an answer, but never guess. If you don't know, write [ASK ME].
My details: [ONLY THE FACTS NEEDED, NO ID NUMBERS]

Watch out

  • You're responsible for every answer on an official form. Don't let an AI guess.
  • Read the official instructions too; the AI may not know the latest version of the form.
  • Remove Social Security numbers, account numbers, member IDs and birth dates before you paste or upload anything. In ChatGPT you can use Temporary Chat or turn off “Improve the model for everyone” under Settings › Data controls.

Questions people ask

Can AI fill out the form for me?

It can draft answers and some agents can type into web forms, but for government, tax, immigration or legal forms you should enter and review every answer yourself.

Which AI tools can do this?

ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or Grok can do this today. Paste the copy-paste prompt on this page into any of them and fill in the [brackets].

How hard is it to set up?

It's rated Medium. Time it saves, by our editor's estimate: 20–40 minutes on a long form. Start with the steps under “What the bot does”.

What should I watch out for?
