Practice speaking a new language out loud with AI

Use an AI voice mode as a patient conversation partner. Pick a situation, like ordering food or checking into a hotel, and talk. It corrects you gently and adapts to your level.

EasyUpdated Oct 24, 2026 · By BotsDoThat · Added Oct 24, 2026

Seen on Hacker News ×3 · YouTube ×2 · Official docs

What the bot does

  1. Plays a role you choose, like a waiter, a shopkeeper or a new friend.
  2. Talks at your level and slows down when you ask.
  3. Corrects mistakes after you speak, without interrupting the flow.
  4. Ends with a short list of words and phrases to review.

What you end up with

Real speaking practice any time, without the nerves of talking to a stranger.

Copy-paste prompt

Fill in the [brackets]. Then paste it into any tool below.

Copy this prompt 
Let's practice [Spanish]. My level is [beginner / A2 / intermediate]. You are [a waiter in a café in Madrid]. Speak only in [Spanish], use short sentences, and speak slowly. After each of my answers, give one correction in English if I made a mistake, then continue. At the end, list 5 useful phrases from our chat.

New to this? How to build an AI agent without coding, or set up a bot in 5 plain steps →

Setup

  1. Open the ChatGPT or Gemini app on your phone and start voice mode (ChatGPT) or Gemini Live.
  2. Say or paste the prompt, filling in the language, level and situation.
  3. Talk for 5 to 10 minutes. Ask it to repeat or slow down whenever you need.
  4. Ask for the phrase list at the end and save it in your notes.

Tools that can do it today

  • chatChatGPTVoice modeFree plan; paid plans add more
  • chatGeminiGemini LiveFree plan; paid plans add more

Plans and limits change; check each tool's page.

Watch out

  • AI can sound confident even when a phrase is unnatural or regional. Check new phrases with a dictionary or a native speaker.
  • Use it alongside a teacher, an app course or real conversations, not as a full replacement.
  • Voice chats may be stored in your account history. Check the app's privacy settings.

What this costs to run

In a chat app it runs inside your plan. If you run it as a bot on an AI API, the bill depends on how often it runs and how much it reads.
