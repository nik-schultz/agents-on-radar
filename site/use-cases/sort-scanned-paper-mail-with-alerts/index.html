Have a bot sort your scanned paper mail and flag what needs you

A virtual mailbox service scans your paper mail to email. The bot reads every letter, queues up the routine ones for your OK, and pulls you in only when something looks unusual.

AdvancedAdded Oct 20, 2026♥ 0 · ↻ 0 on X · measured Oct 22, 2026

What the bot does

  1. A virtual mailbox service opens and scans your paper mail to PDF.
  2. The bot reads each scanned letter in your email.
  3. Sorts it: routine (like a usual monthly payment), needs a reply, or just information.
  4. Compares routine items with past ones and flags anything unusual (the person who shared this uses "more than 20% different").
  5. Queues routine items for your OK and messages you about the rest.

What you end up with

Your paper mail read and sorted, routine items queued for one quick approval, and only the unusual ones in front of you.

Copy-paste prompt

Fill in the [brackets]. Then paste it into any tool below.

Copy this prompt 
Each time a scanned letter arrives in my email from [mail service], read it and add it to my mail log: sender, what it is, any due date, what it asks me to do. If it's a routine item I've approved before, compare it with the last 3 from that sender. If it's more than 20% different, or the sender is new, stop and message me. Otherwise add it to my 'ready to approve' list. Do not pay, reply or sign anything until I approve.
