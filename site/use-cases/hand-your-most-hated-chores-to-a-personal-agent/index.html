Hand your most-hated weekly chores to a personal agent

Personal agents like Meta Muse, Grok Bot and Gemini Spark work in the background on a schedule. Start with the chore you hate most.

EasyAdded Oct 20, 2026♥ 679 · ↻ 35 on X · measured Oct 23, 2026

What the bot does

  1. Asks what you'd like help with and which apps it may use.
  2. Works through the task in the background on its own computer and browser.
  3. Repeats on a schedule you set, like every morning or every Friday.
  4. Messages you only when something needs your decision.

What you end up with

One recurring chore that now happens without you, plus a message when it needs a yes or no.

Copy-paste prompt

Fill in the [brackets]. Then paste it into any tool below.

Copy this prompt 
I want to hand you one boring chore. Here it is: [describe the chore, e.g. 'every Friday, check my email for school forms and deadlines due next week and list them']. Before you start, ask me any questions you need answered. Show me the first result before you set it to repeat. Never send, buy, delete or book anything without asking me first.

Watch out
