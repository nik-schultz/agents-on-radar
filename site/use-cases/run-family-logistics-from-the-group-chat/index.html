🏡 Family & home

Run family logistics from the family group chat

New “family agents” sit in your family chat and handle the small jobs: restocking, booking the dentist and reminders.

EasyAdded Oct 20, 2026♥ 162 · ↻ 14 on X · measured Oct 22, 2026

What the bot does

  1. Lives in your family group chat, so anyone can ask it for something.
  2. Restocks groceries on a shared shopping account.
  3. Books appointments like the dentist.
  4. Sends reminders, for example to Grandma about her meds.

What you end up with

Fewer “did anyone book…?” messages, and one place where family requests get done.

Copy-paste prompt

Fill in the [brackets]. Then paste it into any tool below.

Copy this prompt 
You're our family assistant in this group chat. Jobs you can do: keep a shared shopping list, remind people about appointments, and draft bookings for my approval. Rules: confirm with me before booking or buying anything; never share our address or schedule with anyone outside this chat; reply in one short message.

Watch out

  • Agree as a family what it may book or buy. Set a spending limit.
  • This is a new product category. Coral was announced on Oct 11, 2026, and we haven't tested it.
  • Ask everyone in the chat before adding an agent. It can remember what people post, and not everyone may want that (several X users raised this when Wabi 2.0 launched on Oct 19-21, 2026).
