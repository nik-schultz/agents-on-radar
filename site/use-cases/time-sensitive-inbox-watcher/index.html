Catch offers and deadlines the moment they land in your inbox

For fast-moving work (real estate, sales, hiring), a bot in your inbox and calendar texts you when something time-sensitive arrives.

MediumUpdated Oct 20, 2026 · By BotsDoThat · Added Oct 20, 2026♥ 36 · ↻ 1 on X · measured Oct 23, 2026

What the bot does

  1. Watches your inbox for offers, counteroffers and deadlines.
  2. Texts you who sent it and the key numbers before you open the email.
  3. Builds your follow-up list every Monday.
  4. Flags clashing dates, like a closing that collides with a funding deadline.
  5. Drafts routine emails for your OK.

What you end up with

No missed offers, a Monday follow-up list and early warnings on deadline clashes.

Copy-paste prompt

Fill in the [brackets]. Then paste it into any tool below.

Copy this prompt 
Watch my inbox for offers, counteroffers and deadline changes on my active files: [list]. When one arrives, text me: who, price and terms, and the deadline. Every Monday at 8am, give me a follow-up list of everyone waiting on me. Flag any two dates on my calendar that conflict. Draft replies but never send them.
