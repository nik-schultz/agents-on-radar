Match every business expense to its receipt, weekly

A read-only bot checks your card and payment activity against the receipts in a dedicated inbox and lists what's missing.

AdvancedAdded Oct 20, 2026♥ 68 · ↻ 2 on X · measured Oct 21, 2026

What the bot does

  1. Gets read-only access to your business card activity and a receipts@ inbox.
  2. Every week, matches each charge to a receipt.
  3. Lists charges with no receipt and receipts with no charge.
  4. Flags odd fees, refunds and pending items.

What you end up with

A weekly reconciliation list with the gaps to chase.

Copy-paste prompt

Fill in the [brackets]. Then paste it into any tool below.

Copy this prompt 
Pull this week's business card and payment activity. Match each expense to a receipt in the receipts@ inbox. Give me a table: date, merchant, amount, receipt found (yes/no), notes. List unmatched charges first. You are read-only: never change cards, send payouts or move money.
