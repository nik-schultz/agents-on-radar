Build and test your bot on a free tier before you pay

Several AI providers let you try their API or app for free. Use that to prove your bot works before you put a card on file, and know what you give up on the free tier.

EasyAdded Oct 21, 2026Popularity: not measured

What the bot does

  1. Runs your bot on a free plan or free API tier while you test it.
  2. Uses small, realistic test runs (a day's email, one web page).
  3. Shows you how many tokens each run uses, so you can estimate a real bill.
  4. Moves to a paid plan only once it's doing a job you'd pay for.

What you end up with

A bot you know works, a realistic cost estimate, and no surprise bill while you were experimenting.

Watch out

  • Google says content sent on the Gemini API free tier is used to improve its products; paid-tier content isn't. Don't test with private data on a free tier.
  • Free tiers have lower limits and some models aren't included (Gemini 4 Argon isn't on the free tier).
