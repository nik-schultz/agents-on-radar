Keep a visa application on track with an AI agent

Tell an agent "get my visa by the end of November" and it lists what you need, tracks missing documents and reminds you until it's done. It can also spot an upcoming trip in your calendar and flag visa rules and arrival forms.

EasyUpdated Nov 3, 2026 · By BotsDoThat · Added Nov 3, 2026

Seen on Hacker News · Official docs

What the bot does

  1. Reads your trip dates and destination (from you or your calendar).
  2. Looks up the visa or entry rules on the official government site and lists the documents and steps.
  3. Keeps a checklist of what you still need and reminds you before each deadline.
  4. Flags extra forms such as a digital arrival card and when they open.
  5. Checks in until the visa is approved or you close the task.

What you end up with

One running checklist for your visa and entry forms, with reminders, so nothing is missed before you fly.

Copy-paste prompt

Fill in the [brackets]. Then paste it into any tool below.

Copy this prompt 
I'm travelling to [country] on [dates] with a [nationality] passport for [tourism/business]. Look up the entry and visa rules ONLY on official government websites and link them. Make me a checklist of documents, fees, appointments and deadlines, including any digital arrival card. Every [Monday], remind me what's still missing and what's due next. Don't fill out or submit any form or pay anything without asking me first.

Setup

  1. Open your agent app (Meta Muse, ChatGPT Dots or Grok Bot) and start a new task.
  2. Paste the prompt and fill in the [brackets].
  3. Optional: connect your calendar read-only so it can see trip dates.
  4. Check its checklist against the official government site once yourself.
  5. Say "remind me every Monday" once the checklist looks right.

Watch out

  • Agents can be wrong or out of date: The Straits Times found Muse suggested a restaurant that had already closed. Always confirm visa rules on the official government site.
  • Never let an agent pay visa fees or submit applications on unofficial 'visa service' sites.
  • Some people worry about how much personal data consumer agents collect (see the Hacker News discussion about Muse); connect only the apps you need.

What this costs to run

In a chat app it runs inside your plan. If you run it as a bot on an AI API, the bill depends on how often it runs and how much it reads.
