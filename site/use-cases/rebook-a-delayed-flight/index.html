Get rebooked fast when your flight is delayed

When a flight slips, an agent can find the fastest alternative that still gets you there on time and book it for you.

MediumAdded Oct 20, 2026♥ 17 · ↻ 1 on X · measured Oct 23, 2026

What the bot does

  1. Notices the delay from your airline email or what you tell it.
  2. Searches for the fastest flight that gets you there on time.
  3. Books it, if you've allowed that.
  4. Sends you the new confirmation and a note on what to do with the old ticket.

What you end up with

A new flight confirmation in your chat, without hours on hold or in the app.

Copy-paste prompt

Fill in the [brackets]. Then paste it into any tool below.

Copy this prompt 
My flight [flight number] from [city] to [city] is delayed by [hours]. I need to arrive by [time]. Find the 3 fastest options on any airline, with on-time risk. If I reply with a number, book that one with my saved card. Then tell me what the airline's rules say about my original ticket.

Watch out

  • Decide ahead of time whether it may book without asking. One user's agent did, and it worked out. Yours might not.
  • What happens to the original ticket depends on the airline, the fare and the country.
