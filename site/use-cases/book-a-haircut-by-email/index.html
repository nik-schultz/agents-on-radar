Book a haircut or appointment, even when the form won't work

When the online booking needs a local phone number, an agent can just email the business instead.

EasyAdded Oct 20, 2026♥ 20 · ↻ 4 on X · measured Oct 23, 2026

What the bot does

  1. Finds salons or providers and reads reviews.
  2. Shows you options with photos.
  3. Tries online booking, or writes the business a normal email.
  4. Confirms the slot with you.

What you end up with

A confirmed appointment without the back-and-forth.

Copy-paste prompt

Fill in the [brackets]. Then paste it into any tool below.

Copy this prompt 
Find 3 well-reviewed [salons/barbers/dentists] near [area] that can do [service]. Show me reviews and prices. After I pick, book [day/time range]. If online booking doesn't work, email them asking for that slot, sign it with my name, and say I'm using an assistant. Tell me when it's confirmed.
