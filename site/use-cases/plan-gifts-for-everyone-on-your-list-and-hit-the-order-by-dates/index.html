🛒 Shopping & groceries

Plan gifts for everyone on your list and hit every order-by date

Tell your bot who's on your list, what each person is into and what you want to spend. It suggests a few ideas per person, keeps one tracker of what's picked, ordered, arrived and wrapped, and nudges you each week before the shipping cut-offs, so nobody gets missed and nothing arrives after the holiday.

EasyUpdated Nov 25, 2026 · By BotsDoThat · Added Nov 25, 2026♥ 15 · ↻ 6 on X · measured Nov 27, 2026

Seen on X ×2 · Reddit

What the bot does

  1. Asks for each person's name, age, interests and your budget for them (or splits one total for you).
  2. Suggests 2 or 3 ideas per person (something useful, something fun, an experience) with a link to look at each one.
  3. Builds one tracker: person, idea, link, price, ordered, arrived, wrapped, given, with a running total against your budget.
  4. Notes the latest order date for each item so it arrives in time, and sets reminders a few days before each one.
  5. Checks in once a week until your last shopping day with who still needs a gift and anything over budget. You buy everything yourself.

What you end up with

One list with ideas for everyone, a running total, and a weekly 'still to buy' note, instead of ideas scattered across screenshots, notes and open tabs, and a gift that turns up on December 27.

Copy-paste prompt

Fill in the [brackets]. Then paste it into any tool below.

Copy this prompt 
Help me plan holiday gifts. Here's my list: [name, age, what they're into, budget each, e.g. Mum, 67, gardening and crime novels, $60]. My total is [total budget] and I live in [country].

For each person suggest 3 ideas (one useful, one fun, one experience) with a link and a rough price, and the latest date to order so it arrives by [date]. Add a few small extras for [teachers / neighbours / hosts].

Keep one tracker with: person, idea, link, price, ordered, arrived, wrapped, given, and a running total against my budget. Every [Saturday at 10 a.m.] until [last shopping date], send me who still needs a gift, what's late and anything over budget. Remind me 3 days before each order-by date.

Don't buy anything, don't sign in to any store, and don't share anyone's details. I'll order myself from the links and tell you when something is bought.

Setup

  1. Pick one assistant that can run on a schedule and send you reminders: a Grok Bot, a ChatGPT dot or Tasks in Gemini. A plain chat works too if you only want the ideas and the table.
  2. Write your list first: first names, ages, a few words on what each person likes, and what you want to spend. Set your total yourself before you ask for ideas.
  3. Paste the prompt with your list, your country (for shops and delivery dates) and the day you want to be finished.
  4. Push back on ideas that miss ('she already has every kitchen gadget, try experiences') and pick one per person.
  5. Each time you order something, tell the bot ('bought Mum's book, $24'), so the total and the weekly check-in stay right.

Watch out

  • Order-by dates and prices from a bot are a guide. Check the shop's own delivery cut-off and price before you order (BAARP; the todo.is prompt says the same).
  • Keep it to first names and interests. Don't paste anyone's home address, card numbers or account logins into the chat (BAARP).
  • Some gift bots also watch for price drops or fill carts. If yours does, keep the 'never buy without my yes' rule and a spending cap.
  • AI ideas can be generic. You know the nephew who collects old maps; use the list as a starting point, not the final word.
