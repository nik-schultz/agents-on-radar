Plan a trip itinerary that fits your pace

Planning a trip means 30 open tabs, and you still aren't sure the days fit together.

EasyUpdated Oct 21, 2026 · By BotsDoThat · Added Oct 19, 2026

Seen on YouTube ×2

What the bot does

  1. Fill in the prompt with dates, people, budget and interests.
  2. Ask follow-ups: “make day 2 less walking”, “swap in a kid-friendly lunch”.
  3. Check opening hours and prices on official sites.
  4. Book the urgent items first.
  5. Save the final plan to your phone or a shared doc.

What you end up with

A day-by-day plan with realistic travel times, a budget estimate, and a booking checklist with links to verify.

Copy-paste prompt

Fill in the [brackets]. Then paste it into any tool below.

Copy this prompt 
Plan a [NUMBER]-day trip to [PLACE] for [WHO, AGES] in [MONTH].
Budget: [TOTAL OR PER DAY]. Pace: [RELAXED / BUSY]. We like: [INTERESTS]. Avoid: [THINGS].
Getting there from: [CITY].

1. Day-by-day plan with morning/afternoon/evening, grouped by neighborhood to cut travel time.
2. Rough costs per day, marked as estimates.
3. What to book ahead, in order of urgency.
4. A rainy-day backup for each day.
Tell me what I should double-check (opening hours, prices, closures) because it may be out of date.

Watch out

  • AI can list places that have closed or changed hours. Verify before you go.
  • Prices are estimates. Check the real price at booking.

Questions people ask

Can the AI book the flights and hotel?

Agents like ChatGPT Work can search and prepare bookings on websites and ask before paying. Many people still prefer to book the final step themselves.

Which AI tools can do this?

ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok, ChatGPT Work (cloud browser) or Grok Bot (personal agents) can do this today. Paste the copy-paste prompt on this page into any of them and fill in the [brackets].

How hard is it to set up?

It's rated Easy. Start with the steps under “What the bot does”.

How much time can it save?

In a Censuswide survey of 2,001 employed US adults for Qlik (July 2025), people who used AI to plan their summer trip said it saved them nearly 7 hours of planning on average. That figure is self-reported. Source: Qlik press release: Generative AI Tools are Reshaping Summer Travel.

What should I watch out for?

AI can list places that have closed or changed hours. Verify before you go.
