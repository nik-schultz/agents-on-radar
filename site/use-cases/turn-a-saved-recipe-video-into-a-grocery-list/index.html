🛒 Shopping & groceries

Turn a saved recipe video into a grocery list

Saved a recipe reel or a cooking video you actually want to make? Your bot can pull out the ingredients and steps, leave out what you already have, and save a shopping list sorted by aisle to your notes. It reads and writes the list; it doesn't order anything unless you say so.

EasyUpdated Nov 27, 2026 · By BotsDoThat · Added Nov 27, 2026♥ 17 · ↻ 0 on X · measured Nov 27, 2026

Seen on X · Official docs

What the bot does

  1. Opens the recipe video you point it to (a reel you saved on Instagram, a YouTube link, or a page) and lists the ingredients with amounts.
  2. Scales the amounts to the number of people you're cooking for and drops anything you say you already have.
  3. Sorts what's left into a shopping list by store section (produce, dairy, pantry...).
  4. Saves the list where you'll see it in the store, for example a Google Keep note, your phone's notes or your usual grocery list.
  5. Writes the steps as a short numbered recipe with a link back to the creator, and marks any amount it couldn't hear or see as 'check video'.

What you end up with

A ready shopping list on your phone and a short written recipe, both made from the video you saved, with any unclear amounts flagged instead of guessed.

Copy-paste prompt

Fill in the [brackets]. Then paste it into any tool below.

Copy this prompt 
Here's a recipe video I want to make: [paste the link, or say 'the recipe reel I saved most recently'].

1. List every ingredient with its amount, scaled for [number] people.
2. Leave out what I already have: [salt, olive oil, eggs...].
3. Sort the rest into a shopping list by store section (produce, meat, dairy, pantry, frozen).
4. Save the list as [a new Google Keep note called '[dish] shopping' / a note on my phone / my grocery list].
5. Write the steps as a short numbered recipe in my notes, with the creator's name and a link to the video.

If an amount isn't said or shown in the video, write 'check video' instead of guessing. Flag anything that contains [allergies, e.g. nuts]. Don't order or buy anything for me.

Setup

  1. Pick the app you already use. Muse works with your Instagram saves; Gemini works with YouTube links and Google Keep; any bot with a browser can read a link you paste.
  2. Muse: Instagram connects automatically if both are in the same Meta Accounts Center. Gemini: connect Google Workspace and keep Gemini Apps history on so it can save to Keep.
  3. Paste the prompt with your recipe link, serving size and what's already in your kitchen.
  4. Open the saved list and check it against the video once, especially amounts for baking.
  5. Happy with it? Ask the bot to do the same every time you save a recipe, or each Sunday for the recipes you saved that week.

Watch out

  • Say 'save a new note in Google Keep called...' to Gemini. In one test the plain wording only showed a checklist in the chat and saved nothing (TheNextGenAI, Nov 19).
  • Watch the video before relying on the quantities. Bots can mishear amounts or miss ones only shown on screen (TheNextGenAI), and Google's Gemini help says Gemini can hallucinate or give outdated information.
  • Muse can use what you can see on Facebook, Instagram and Threads once they share an Accounts Center (Meta). Check what's connected there, and leave out accounts you don't need, such as a business account.
  • Keep 'don't order anything' in the prompt unless you want the bot to fill a cart, and check the cart yourself before you pay.

What this costs to run

In a chat app it runs inside your plan. If you run it as a bot on an AI API, the bill depends on how often it runs and how much it reads.
