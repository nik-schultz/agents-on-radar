Log your meals by talking and get a daily weight check-in

Tell your bot what you ate, out loud, and it keeps the food log for you. It can also check in on your weight each morning.

EasyUpdated Oct 20, 2026 · By BotsDoThat · Added Oct 20, 2026♥ 66 · ↻ 3 on X · measured Oct 28, 2026

Seen on X · YouTube

What the bot does

  1. Listens when you say what you ate and writes it into a simple food log.
  2. Estimates the basics you care about, like calories or protein, and labels them as estimates.
  3. Checks in each morning about your weight (or reads it from your scale's app if you connect it).
  4. Sends a short weekly summary of trends.

What you end up with

A food and weight log that fills itself in, without typing into a tracking app.

Copy-paste prompt

Fill in the [brackets]. Then paste it into any tool below.

Copy this prompt 
Be my food and weight log. When I tell you what I ate, add it to a log with the date, time and a rough estimate of [calories / protein]; label estimates as estimates. Every morning at [time], ask me for my weight and add it. Every [Sunday], send me a short summary of the week's trends. Don't give medical advice; tell me to ask my doctor if something looks off.

Watch out

  • Calorie and nutrition numbers from a description are rough estimates.
  • This is health data. Check what your bot's app stores and who can see it.
  • It's not a doctor. Talk to one before big diet changes, especially with a health condition.

What this costs to run

In a chat app it runs inside your plan. If you run it as a bot on an AI API, the bill depends on how often it runs and how much it reads.
