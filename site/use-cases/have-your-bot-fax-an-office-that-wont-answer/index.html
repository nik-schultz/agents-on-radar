Have your bot send a fax when an office won't pick up the phone

Some doctor's offices and agencies are almost impossible to reach by phone, and some hang up on AI callers. Your bot can find their fax number, write a one-page request in your name, and send it through an online fax service once you approve it.

EasyUpdated Nov 23, 2026 · By BotsDoThat · Added Nov 19, 2026

Seen on GitHub · Official docs

What the bot does

  1. Confirms the office's phone and fax numbers on its own website.
  2. Tries the phone first if you want, asking you before each call.
  3. If the calls fail, writes a short one-page request: who you are, what you need, how to reach you and when you're free.
  4. Shows you the page and waits for your OK.
  5. Sends it through an online fax service connected to your chat app, then checks whether it went through.

What you end up with

Your request sitting on the office's fax machine, plus a copy and a delivery status, without buying a fax machine or driving over.

Copy-paste prompt

Fill in the [brackets]. Then paste it into any tool below.

Copy this prompt 
I need [what you need, e.g. an appointment with Dr. ___ to sign some paperwork] from [office name] in [town].

1. Confirm the office phone and fax numbers on the office's own website and show me the link.
2. [Optional] Ask me before each call, then try calling.
3. If you can't get through, draft a one-page fax in my name: what I need, my phone number, and the times I'm free: [times]. Keep health details to the minimum.
4. Show me the page. Only send it after I say yes.
5. Tell me when it was delivered, or if it failed.

Setup

  1. Add a fax connector to your chat app. Faxwell and GotFreeFax work in Claude without an account; Faxify connects to ChatGPT and Claude. A free fax may need you to click a confirmation link in your email.
  2. Paste the prompt and fill in the brackets.
  3. Check the fax number it found against the office's own website before you approve.
  4. Read the page it drafted, then approve the send.
  5. Ask 'did my fax go through?' an hour later, and call or use the patient portal if you hear nothing in a few days.

Watch out

  • The document passes through your AI app and the fax service. Don't include your full medical history, ID numbers or insurance numbers unless the office asks for them.
  • Free fax services have page and daily limits, and some ask you to confirm each fax by email.
  • In the source, the nurse hung up when she heard a virtual assistant. Be ready for that, and use the patient portal if the office has one.

What this costs to run

In a chat app it runs inside your plan. If you run it as a bot on an AI API, the bill depends on how often it runs and how much it reads.
