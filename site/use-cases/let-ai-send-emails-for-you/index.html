Let an AI write and send your emails, after you OK each one

Yes. ChatGPT and Claude can now send email from your connected Gmail (ChatGPT also Outlook), and Gemini's Tasks can reply and forward. Each one shows you the email and waits for your OK before it goes out, unless you switch that off. Keep it on.

EasyUpdated Nov 3, 2026 · By BotsDoThat · Added Nov 3, 2026

Seen on Official docs

What the bot does

  1. Reads the thread or the notes you give it (who it's to, what you want to say, the tone).
  2. Writes the email in your voice, with the To, Cc and Subject lines filled in.
  3. Shows you the full draft and an approval card (Allow or Deny) before anything is sent.
  4. Sends it from your own account once you approve, or saves it as a draft if you'd rather press Send yourself.
  5. Tells you what it sent and to whom, so you can check your Sent folder.

What you end up with

Emails written and sent from your own Gmail or Outlook without opening your inbox, each one checked and approved by you first.

Copy this prompt 
Write an email to [name, email address] about [what it's about]. Here's what I want to say: [2-3 bullet points]. Tone: [friendly / formal / short]. Sign it [your name]. Show me the full email with To, Cc and Subject before you do anything. Do not send it until I reply "send it". Only send to the addresses I gave you, never add anyone, and don't attach or forward anything. If you're not sure about a fact, leave a [CHECK] note in the draft instead of guessing.

Setup

  1. Connect your email with the app's official connect button, never by pasting your password into the chat. In ChatGPT: Settings > Apps, then add Gmail or Outlook. In Claude: Customize > Connectors, then Gmail.
  2. Check the permission setting before your first send. ChatGPT's options run from "Always ask" to "Allow all actions"; Claude lets you set each Gmail tool to Always allow, Needs approval or Blocked. Pick the one that asks before sending.
  3. Want drafts only? In Claude, set the send, reply and forward tools to Blocked, so it can write drafts but never send. In Gmail itself, Gemini's Help me write drafts the email and you press Send.
  4. Send your first test email to yourself, then check your Sent folder to see exactly what went out.

Watch out

  • Don't pick "Allow all actions" or "Always allow" for sending. A sent email can't be pulled back, and OpenAI labels that setting as elevated risk.
  • Read the To and Cc lines, not just the text. A wrong autocomplete on a name sends your email to the wrong person.
  • TechRadar's June 2026 test found ChatGPT couldn't attach files when sending, and Claude's Gmail connector sees only that a file is attached, not what's in it. Send attachments yourself.
  • Emails from strangers can hide instructions meant for your AI. Don't let it reply to or forward messages from people you don't know without reading them first.
  • Sending is often web-only or paid-only. ChatGPT sends only on the web on paid plans; on Free or Go it can write the email but not send it.

What this costs to run

In a chat app it runs inside your plan. If you run it as a bot on an AI API, the bill depends on how often it runs and how much it reads.

Questions people ask

Can ChatGPT send emails for me?

Yes, if you connect Gmail or Outlook. Since June 2026, ChatGPT on the web can draft and send email from your account on the Plus, Pro, Business and Enterprise plans. It shows the email and asks you to Allow or Deny before it sends. It doesn't work on Free or Go plans.

Can Claude send emails on my behalf?

Yes. Anthropic's help page says Claude's Gmail connector can send, reply to and forward emails, and asks for your approval before each of these actions by default. On Team and Enterprise plans, owners decide whether members can turn that prompt off.

Can Gemini send emails for me?

Gemini in Gmail writes drafts and you press Send. Tasks in the Gemini app can reply to and forward Gmail threads, and Google says it always asks for your confirmation before sending. Tasks aren't available in the EEA, the UK, Switzerland or Nigeria.

Can AI send emails automatically, without me?

Some can if you turn approvals off, but we don't recommend it. ChatGPT's tasks can react to new Gmail messages on paid plans, and any action that needs approval pauses until you review it. Keep sending on "ask first".

Which AI tools can do this?

ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini Spark or Grok Bot (personal agents) can do this today. Paste the copy-paste prompt on this page into any of them and fill in the [brackets].
