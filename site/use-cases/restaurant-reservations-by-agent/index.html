Book restaurant tables by message, even at phone-only places

Restaurant bookings are the gateway task for many agent users, including small places that only take WhatsApp or calls.

EasyAdded Oct 20, 2026♥ 533 · ↻ 23 on X · measured Oct 21, 2026

What the bot does

  1. Takes where you'll be, when, and what you like.
  2. Finds places nearby and checks availability.
  3. Books online, or messages the restaurant if there's no booking site.
  4. Sends you the confirmation.

What you end up with

A table booked and a confirmation in your chat.

Copy-paste prompt

Fill in the [brackets]. Then paste it into any tool below.

Copy this prompt 
Book dinner for [number] people on [date] around [time] near [address]. I like [cuisine], budget about [$ per person]. Give me 3 options first. After I pick, book it under my name, and tell me the cancellation policy.

Tools that can do it today

  • agentGemini + OpenTableBook, cancel or check OpenTable reservationsWorks in the Gemini app
  • agentInstinctWhatsApps small restaurantsInvite-only beta (per WIRED, Sep 2026)
  • agentMeta MuseCan call restaurantsFree with a usage limit; paid tier for more
  • agentGrok Bot (personal agents)Check plan requirements in xAI's docs

Plans and limits change; check each tool's page.

Watch out

  • It can be slower than doing it yourself. One startup timed agents taking 4 to 14+ minutes for a dinner booking; the founder did it in 37 seconds.
  • Don't let it hold tables you won't use. WIRED reports one investor got banned from Resy after his bot hit it about 200 times an hour.
