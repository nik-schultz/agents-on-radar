Make a moving checklist and change your address everywhere

Moving means telling dozens of companies and offices your new address, and something always gets missed. Your AI finds who to tell from your inbox, then an agent works through the address-change pages with you, asking before anything final.

EasyUpdated Nov 3, 2026 · By BotsDoThat · Added Oct 19, 2026♥ 7 · ↻ 1 on X · measured Nov 6, 2026

Seen on X · Official docs

What the bot does

  1. Searches your email for bills, statements, receipts and subscriptions, and lists every company that likely has your address.
  2. Makes a week-by-week moving checklist, then asks you before it puts utility stop and start dates on your Google Calendar.
  3. In ChatGPT Work's cloud browser, opens each company's website, pauses while you sign in yourself, fills in your new address and asks you before it saves or submits.
  4. Leaves the USPS change of address to you, on the official USPS site, for the official fee.
  5. Keeps a done and not-done list, so you can see what's still open a month after the move.

What you end up with

A personal moving checklist by week, a list of everyone to notify built from your own inbox, address changes done with you approving each one, and the official USPS change of address done for the official fee.

Copy-paste prompt

Fill in the [brackets]. Then paste it into any tool below.

Copy this prompt 
I'm moving from [CITY/STATE] to [CITY/STATE] on [DATE]. My household: [WHO, PETS, CAR?]
My new address: [NEW ADDRESS]

1. Search my email from the last 12 months for bills, statements, receipts and subscriptions. List every company that likely has my address, grouped (government, money, utilities, health, subscriptions, work/school). Leave out account numbers.
2. Make a week-by-week moving checklist starting today, including which utilities to stop or transfer and when. Ask me first, then add the stop and start dates to my Google Calendar.
3. For each company I pick from the list: open its website, let me sign in myself, and fill in my new address. Stop and ask me before you save or submit anything.
4. Don't do the USPS change of address. I'll do that myself on usps.com.
5. Keep a list of what's done and what's still open.

Setup

  1. For steps 1 and 2, use Claude: click + under the chat box, hover over Connectors and turn on Gmail and Google Calendar.
  2. For step 3, open ChatGPT, start a task in Work, and paste in the list of companies you picked plus your new address.
  3. When a site needs a login, sign in through ChatGPT's secure sign-in form. Never type a password or card number into the chat.
  4. Do the USPS change of address yourself at usps.com.

Tools that can do it today

  • chatClaudeSteps 1 and 2. Turn on the Gmail and Google Calendar connectors (free plan works). It asks before each calendar event by default.Free plan; paid plans add more
  • agentChatGPT Work (cloud browser)Step 3. Its cloud browser fills in forms on websites, pauses for you to sign in through a secure form, and asks before actions that are hard to undo. Signing in to websites is on Plus and Pro.Paid ChatGPT plans only (not Free or Go); rolling out
  • chatChatGPTAny plan: paste a list of your bills (company names only) for the checklist and the list of who to tell.Free plan; paid plans add more

Plans and limits change; check each tool's page.

Watch out

  • USPS warns that some third-party sites charge $40 or more to file a change of address. The official online fee is $1.25 for identity verification.
  • Never type passwords, security codes or card numbers into the chat. ChatGPT Work has a secure sign-in form for websites.
  • Check each address before you approve it. A typo here sends your mail to the wrong place.
  • The cloud browser works only on websites ChatGPT supports. Do the rest yourself from the list.

Questions people ask

Which AI tools can do this?

Use Claude with Gmail and Google Calendar connected to find who to tell and set the dates, and ChatGPT Work's cloud browser (paid plans) to fill in the address forms with you approving. Any chat app, ChatGPT's free plan included, can make the checklist from a list you paste.

When can I file the USPS change of address?

USPS says you can submit it up to 90 days before your move and up to 30 days after.

Can an AI agent change my address for me?

On many websites, yes, with you approving. ChatGPT Work's cloud browser can fill in forms, pauses so you can sign in, and asks before things that are hard to undo. OpenAI's own examples include setting up utilities for a new apartment. Do the USPS change yourself on usps.com.

How hard is it to set up?

It's rated Easy. Start with the steps under “What the bot does”.

What should I watch out for?

USPS warns that some third-party sites charge $40 or more to file a change of address. The official online fee is $1.25 for identity verification.
