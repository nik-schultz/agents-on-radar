Make a moving checklist and change your address everywhere

Moving means telling dozens of companies and offices your new address, and something always gets missed.

EasyUpdated Oct 19, 2026 · By BotsDoThat · Added Oct 19, 2026♥ 7 · ↻ 1 on X · measured Oct 23, 2026

What the bot does

  1. Fill in the prompt with your move date and accounts.
  2. Do the USPS change of address on the official USPS site only.
  3. Work through the AI's list, ticking off each organization.
  4. Set reminders for utility stop/start dates.
  5. A month after the move, ask the AI which items are still open.

What you end up with

A personal moving checklist by week, a list of everyone to notify, and the official USPS change of address done for the official fee.

Copy-paste prompt

Fill in the [brackets]. Then paste it into any tool below.

Copy this prompt 
I'm moving from [CITY/STATE] to [CITY/STATE] on [DATE]. My household: [WHO, PETS, CAR?]
Here are my recurring bills/accounts (no account numbers): [LIST OR PASTE A STATEMENT]

1. Make a week-by-week moving checklist starting today.
2. List every organization I should give my new address to, grouped (government, money, utilities, health, subscriptions, work/school).
3. Tell me which utilities to stop or transfer and when.
4. Write a short, reusable message I can send to update my address.

Watch out

  • USPS warns that some third-party sites charge $40 or more to file a change of address. The official online fee is $1.25 for identity verification.
  • Never give an AI your account numbers; just the company names.

Questions people ask

When can I file the USPS change of address?

USPS says you can submit it up to 90 days before your move and up to 30 days after.

Which AI tools can do this?
