🛒 Shopping & groceries

Have your bot check on every package and chase the late ones

Your bot finds every shipping email from the last couple of weeks, opens each carrier's tracking page, and tells you what's on time, what's stuck and what to do about it. If a package is late or marked delivered but missing, it drafts the message to the store (or, in some apps, calls them) and waits for your OK.

EasyUpdated Nov 20, 2026 · By BotsDoThat · Added Nov 20, 2026♥ 48 · ↻ 1 on X · measured Nov 23, 2026

Seen on X ×2

What the bot does

  1. Searches your email for order and shipping confirmations from the last [2] weeks and pulls out each tracking number.
  2. Opens each carrier's tracking page itself, because the status in a days-old email is often out of date.
  3. Sorts everything into: arriving today, on its way, stuck or late, and delivered.
  4. For anything stuck (for example sitting at 'label created' for days) or 'delivered' but not at your door, drafts a short message to the store with the order number, and asks you before sending.
  5. Can run on a schedule, like every morning in December, and only message you when something needs you.

What you end up with

One short list of every package and where it really is, plus a ready-to-send note to the store for any that went wrong.

Copy-paste prompt

Fill in the [brackets]. Then paste it into any tool below.

Copy this prompt 
Check on all my packages. Look through my email for order and shipping confirmations from the last [14] days.

For each one, open the carrier's tracking page (don't just trust the email) and give me one short list:
- Arriving today
- On its way (with the expected date)
- Stuck or late (no movement for [3] days, or past the promised date)
- Delivered

For anything stuck, late, or marked delivered that I say I don't have, draft a short, polite message to the store with the order number, what the tracking shows, and what I'd like (an update or a replacement). Show me the draft. Don't send, call or file a claim without asking me first.

If you can't open a tracking page, say so instead of guessing.

Setup

  1. Pick an assistant that can read your email and open websites: Tasks in Gemini (switch the Gemini home screen from Chat to Task), Grok Bot, ChatGPT dots or Muse.
  2. Connect only the email account your orders go to. Read-only is enough for the checking part.
  3. Paste the prompt and run it once while you watch. Check that it found every order you know about.
  4. If it works, make it a routine: every morning during the holidays, or every Monday. Ask it to stay quiet unless something is late.
  5. When it drafts a message to a store, read it, fix anything wrong, then send it yourself or tell it to send.

Watch out

  • A routine can quietly stop working. One Grok Bot user found his shipping-tracking bot had 'silently ceased to do any work at all' after a one-off 'don't retry that' instruction during a UPS outage (X @randallwdean, Nov 16). Ask it to send a one-line 'all checked' note so you notice when it goes silent.
  • It only sees orders that have an email in the inbox you connected. Orders placed under another email or in an app won't show up.
  • Calling a store or filing a missing-package claim is a real action. Keep 'ask me first' on, and check the order number before it goes out.
  • Shipping-scam texts are common in the holidays. The bot should use the tracking link from the store's own email or the carrier's site, never a link from a text.
