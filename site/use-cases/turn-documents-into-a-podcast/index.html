Turn a pile of documents into a podcast you can listen to

Upload PDFs, notes or links to Gemini Notebook (formerly NotebookLM) and it makes an audio overview: two AI hosts talking through your material, ready to play on a walk or in the car.

EasyUpdated Oct 24, 2026 · By BotsDoThat · Added Oct 24, 2026

Seen on Hacker News ×2 · YouTube ×2 · Official docs

What the bot does

  1. Reads the documents, links or notes you add to a notebook.
  2. Writes a conversation between two AI hosts that covers the key points.
  3. Turns it into audio you can play at the speed you like.
  4. Answers follow-up questions from the same sources, with citations.

What you end up with

A listenable summary of a long report, a course reading or a stack of meeting notes.

Copy-paste prompt

Fill in the [brackets]. Then paste it into any tool below.

Copy this prompt 
(Type this in the Audio Overview customise box.) Focus on [the three decisions in this report / what a beginner needs to know / the risks]. My level is [new to this / I know the basics]. Keep it [shorter / default]. Point out anything the sources disagree on.

Setup

  1. Go to notebooklm.google and sign in with a Google account.
  2. Create a notebook and add your sources: PDFs, Google Docs, website links or pasted text.
  3. In the Studio panel, choose Audio Overview. Pick a format (Deep Dive, Brief, Critique or Debate) and a language.
  4. Optionally type what to focus on, then generate. It takes a few minutes.
  5. Play it, and ask the notebook chat to show the source for anything surprising.

Tools that can do it today

  • chatGemini Notebook (formerly NotebookLM)Audio Overview, in the Studio panelFree with a Google account; Google AI plans raise usage limits

Plans and limits change; check each tool's page.

Watch out

  • Google's help page says Audio Overviews are AI-generated and may contain inaccuracies or audio glitches. Check facts in the sources before you rely on them.
  • Only upload documents you're allowed to share with Google. Leave out private medical, legal or financial papers unless you're comfortable with that.
  • Free accounts have usage limits that refresh over time; heavier use needs a Google AI plan.

What this costs to run

In a chat app it runs inside your plan. If you run it as a bot on an AI API, the bill depends on how often it runs and how much it reads.
