Beginner guide · Personal AI agents

ChatGPT dots vs Grok Bot vs Meta Muse (plus Claude Cowork and Gemini)

Short answer: use the personal agent your plan already includes. ChatGPT dots come with ChatGPT Pro, Grok Bot with X Premium, Super or Ultra, Claude Cowork with any paid Claude plan. Tasks in Gemini is free in the US, Canada, India, Japan, South Korea and Australia (Google AI Pro or Ultra elsewhere), and Meta Muse has a free tier. All five can work on jobs for you, not just chat, and all five let you require your OK before they act.

Which one should you pick?

  • You pay for nothing yet: tasks in Gemini if you're in the US, Canada, India, Japan, South Korea or Australia (how to use Tasks in Gemini), or Meta Muse (free with a usage limit). Good first jobs: have your bot make a customer-service phone call for you or add an AI to your friends' group chat to make plans happen.
  • You already pay for ChatGPT Pro: a dot. Try have your bot catch calendar clashes hiding in your email or let ChatGPT catch you up on your iCloud Mail and update your iPhone calendar.
  • You want several agents with separate jobs that hand work to each other: Grok Bot. Try share a scam-check bot your parent can text before they send money or let your bot sign in without seeing your passwords.
  • Your work lives in files and documents: Claude Cowork. Try turn scattered booking emails into one trip sheet that flags clashes or put together a home or car insurance claim with an AI agent, and read keep an AI agent from deleting your files first.
  • You live in Gmail and Google Calendar: tasks in Gemini (step-by-step setup). Try plan a week of family meals and fill the grocery cart or get alerted the moment your airline changes your flight.
  • You'd rather run your own: open-source agents, compared in OpenClaw vs Hermes Agent and OpenClaw alternatives.

How to start with any personal AI agent: 6 steps

  1. Pick one boring, repeating job. Choose something you do every week that only needs reading, like an inbox check or a calendar clash check. Read-only first; sending and buying later.
  2. Use the agent your plan already includes. Check the table: if you already pay for ChatGPT Pro, Claude, Google AI Pro or an X subscription, start there. If you pay for none, try tasks in Gemini (free in six countries) or Meta Muse's free tier.
  3. Connect only the apps that job needs. One job, one or two apps. Don't connect banking, health or work accounts on day one.
  4. Paste a short first-job prompt. Say the job, what it may use, what it must never do, when to ask you, and what the result looks like. Copy the prompt below.
  5. Keep approvals on. Make sure sending, buying, deleting and posting need your OK. Every agent on this page has a setting or rule for that.
  6. Run it once by hand, then schedule it. Check the first result yourself. Only when it's right, ask the agent to repeat it on a schedule.

Copy this first job into any of the five. It only reads, so it's a safe first test:

First job for any personal AI agent 
Your job: every weekday at 7 AM, read my email from the last 24 hours and send me a short list of
1) what needs a reply today, 2) deadlines this week, 3) anything that looks like a scam.
Use only: my email, read-only.
Never: send, delete, archive, buy, sign up or post anything.
Ask me first: before any action that changes something or contacts anyone.
If unsure: say what you're unsure about instead of guessing.
Result: at most 10 bullets, most important first, each with the sender and one line on why.

Before you connect your email

  1. Start read-only. Let it send, buy or delete only after a week of good results.
  2. Keep approvals on for sending, buying, deleting and posting.
  3. Never type passwords or card numbers into the chat; use the agent's own sign-in feature or do that step yourself.
  4. Treat emails and web pages it reads as untrusted: is it safe to give an AI agent your email?

Questions

Which personal AI agent is best for beginners?

Start with the one your current plan already includes, because the job matters more than the brand. If you pay for ChatGPT Pro, try a dot; if you pay for Claude, try Cowork; with an X Premium, Super or Ultra subscription, try Grok Bot. If you pay for none, tasks in Gemini is free in the US, Canada, India, Japan, South Korea and Australia, and Meta Muse has a free tier with a usage limit.

Is there a free personal AI agent?

Yes. Tasks in Gemini became free on Nov 17, 2026 in the US, Canada, India, Japan, South Korea and Australia (Google AI Pro or Ultra elsewhere), and Meta Muse is free with a usage limit. ChatGPT dots, Claude Cowork and Grok Bot need a paid plan. Open-source agents such as Hermes Agent and OpenClaw are free to download, but you pay for the AI model and the computer they run on.

Do personal AI agents keep working when my computer is off?

ChatGPT dots and Grok Bot work on their own cloud computers, so yes. Claude Cowork's scheduled tasks run in the cloud with no device online, but jobs that use your local files or browser need the desktop app open. Tasks in Gemini that use your own Chrome need your computer awake; otherwise Gemini may use a remote browser.

Is it safe to let a personal agent read my email?

It can be, with limits: connect only what the job needs, start read-only, and keep approvals on for sending, buying and deleting. Emails can contain hidden instructions aimed at agents, so treat what it reads as untrusted. All five agents here have approval settings, and Meta and Google both warn their agents can make mistakes.

What is the difference between ChatGPT and ChatGPT dots?

ChatGPT answers when you ask. A dot is an always-on agent inside ChatGPT with its own cloud computer and browser: it works on tasks in the background, connects to your apps and messages you with progress or questions. Dots are on ChatGPT Pro and Business Premium in eligible markets.

Can I use more than one personal agent?

Yes, but give each one a different job and don't connect the same accounts to all of them. Two agents watching one inbox means twice the access and twice the chance of a mistake.
