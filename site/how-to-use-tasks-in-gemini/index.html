How to use Tasks in Gemini (now free): your first task, step by step

Short answer: open Gemini on your phone, Mac or gemini.google.com, flip the toggle next to the text box from Chat to Task, describe the job and when it should run, and submit. Gemini then works on it for you: it can browse websites, work in Gmail, Calendar and Drive, and repeat the job on a schedule. Since Nov 17, 2026 it's free with a personal Google account in the US, Canada, India, Japan, South Korea and Australia (Google; Gemini Help). It used to be called Gemini Spark.

Updated Nov 19, 2026 · By BotsDoThat

Every fact comes from Google's own help pages and announcement, linked inline and checked Nov 19, 2026. Google is rolling this out in stages and changing it often.

What Tasks in Gemini is

  • Chat answers; Task does. In Task mode Gemini works on a job in the background, using your Connected Apps, websites you're signed into, your files and more, and shows its plan and progress as it goes (Gemini Help).
  • Three building blocks. A task is the goal, a schedule says when it runs (a time, or an event like a new email), and a skill is reusable instructions for how to do it (Gemini Help).
  • It can use a browser. Either your own Chrome on a computer (which must stay awake) or a separate remote browser in the cloud. Some steps, like signing in, pause until you take over.
  • New in the US: it can phone businesses, pay with Google Pay after you approve, and take jobs by email or text (Google).

How to set up your first task: 7 steps

  1. Check you can use it. You need to be 18 or over, signed in with a personal Google account (not a work or school one), with Gemini History on. It's free in the US, Canada, India, Japan, South Korea and Australia; elsewhere it needs Google AI Pro or Ultra, and it isn't offered in the EEA, UK, Switzerland or Nigeria.
  2. Switch from Chat to Task. Open the Gemini app on your phone, the Gemini app on a Mac, or gemini.google.com. Flip the toggle next to the text box from Chat to Task. No toggle? Re-check step 1; Google is also rolling it out in stages.
  3. Let Gemini interview you (optional). Type “get started”, “set up” or “interview me” in a task and Gemini walks you through your first tasks and skills.
  4. Describe one read-only job. Say what to do, what it may use, what it must never do, and what the result looks like. Start with a job that only reads, like an inbox check. Copy the prompt below.
  5. Connect only the apps that job needs. Gemini can use Connected Apps such as Gmail, Calendar, Drive and Keep. For a first job, Gmail alone is enough.
  6. Watch the first run. Open the task thread and click the progress chip to see each step. Type new instructions at any time; Gemini changes course without starting over. If it asks you to take over the browser, do that step yourself.
  7. Then put it on a schedule. Ask in the task thread (“do this every morning”) or open Scheduled in the sidebar and click Create a scheduled task. Use More, then Run now to test it. Check a few runs before you trust it.

Paste this as your first task. It only reads, so it's a safe test:

First task for Gemini (read-only) 
Task: every morning, read my Gmail from the last 24 hours and give me a short list of
1) emails that need a reply today, 2) dates or deadlines this week, 3) anything that looks like a scam.
Use only: my Gmail, read-only.
Never: send, reply, delete, archive, unsubscribe, buy or sign up for anything.
Ask me first: before any step that changes something or contacts anyone.
If unsure: say what you're unsure about instead of guessing.
Result: at most 10 bullets, most important first, each with the sender and one line on why.

Want a different job? Build it in the free instruction builder and paste the result into a task, or use the 6-part instructions template. Sources: Manage your tasks, Schedules for tasks.

Hand Gemini a task by email or text (US)

  • Email: turn on Email with Gemini on the Channels page (gemini.google.com/channels), then forward or send emails to gemini@google.com from Gmail, Apple Mail or Outlook. Gemini replies only to you, with a link to review and confirm the result (9to5Google).
  • Text: in the Gemini app on Android or iPhone, tap Settings, then Channels, then Set up under Text with Gemini, and verify your phone number. You need a US number with an eligible carrier (Google). Google notes these texts are not end-to-end encrypted (9to5Google).
  • Phone calls: ask it to call businesses, for example to find an appointment. Not yet in Indiana, Louisiana, Minnesota, Montana or Nebraska (Google). More: can AI make phone calls for you?

Good first tasks to try

  • Inbox: get your email inbox under control, turn the flood of school emails into one weekly digest or the Morning Inbox Briefing blueprint.
  • Family calendar: catch family calendar clashes before the week starts or have a bot watch your kids' team apps for plan changes.
  • Travel: get alerted the moment your airline changes your flight. Google's help also suggests watching flight and hotel prices within your budget (Gemini Help).
  • News: get a short morning digest on topics you care about.

Before you let it act: 6 rules

  1. Never type passwords or card numbers into a task. When it needs them, take over the browser and enter them on the website yourself (Gemini Help).
  2. Don't schedule sensitive jobs. Google warns that if a schedule runs while you're offline, you may not be able to stop an unintended action.
  3. Keep confirmations on. Google says Gemini is designed to always ask before sending messages, deleting data, buying, paying or submitting forms, even if you chose “Always allow” for routine steps.
  4. Watch Google Tasks. Google's help says Gemini can add, change and delete many reminders in Google Tasks at once without asking, so word those requests carefully.
  5. Treat what it reads as untrusted. Emails and web pages can hide instructions meant for the agent (prompt injection). See is it safe to give an AI agent your email?
  6. Clean up after sensitive jobs. Settings, then Browser, then Delete remote browser data. To stop everything: Settings, then Task settings, then Turn off. Schedules pause and your tasks and files are kept; remote browser data is deleted.

Questions

Is Gemini Tasks free?

Yes, in six countries. Since Nov 17, 2026, tasks in Gemini is free without a Google AI subscription in the US, Canada, India, Japan, South Korea and Australia. Elsewhere you need Google AI Pro or Ultra, and it isn't available in the EEA, UK, Switzerland or Nigeria. Free use has a compute-based usage limit and schedules can run at most once a day.

What happened to Gemini Spark?

Google renamed it. Gemini's help page says Spark was tested in beta with subscribers since May and renamed Task because people kept asking it to finish tasks. Your existing tasks and schedules are still in the sidebar.

Why can't I see the Task option in Gemini?

Check four things: you're 18 or over, signed in with a personal Google account (work and school accounts can't use it yet), History is on, and you're in a supported country. Google is rolling it out in stages, so it may not have reached every eligible account yet.

Can Gemini run tasks on a schedule for free?

Yes, up to once a day in a general time window (overnight, daytime or evening), with up to 10 active schedules. Exact times, hourly runs, Gmail triggers and topic monitors need Google AI Pro or Ultra.

Does Gemini keep working when my computer is off?

Mostly. Google's help says Gemini runs tasks in the cloud and keeps working when your device is off. Tasks that use your own Chrome browser need your computer awake; if it isn't, Gemini may switch to a remote browser, and some steps, like signing in, wait for you.

Can Gemini make phone calls and buy things for me?

In the US, yes. It can call businesses (not yet in Indiana, Louisiana, Minnesota, Montana or Nebraska) and says it's an AI. It can pay with Google Pay; Google says it's designed to ask for your confirmation or pre-approval before clicking Buy, and you can set a spending cap.

How do I email or text a task to Gemini?

In the US, open the Channels page (in the Gemini app: Settings, then Channels; on the web: gemini.google.com/channels). Turn on Email with Gemini, then forward or send emails to gemini@google.com; or set up Text with Gemini and verify your phone number. Texts aren't end-to-end encrypted, so don't text anything private.

Is it safe to let Gemini work in my Gmail?

Start read-only and keep confirmations on. Google says Gemini is designed to always ask before sending messages, deleting data, buying or submitting forms, but warns that emails and web pages can hide instructions aimed at the agent (prompt injection). Don't schedule sensitive tasks, and never type passwords or card numbers into a task.
