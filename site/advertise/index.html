What we measure and share with sponsors

Questions

How many visitors do you have?

We launched on Oct 21, 2026, so the honest answer is: not many yet. We don't publish traffic numbers we haven't measured. When Search Console has data we will share impressions and clicks with you before you pay.

Will a sponsorship change your rankings or prices?

No. Trending is computed from public engagement numbers and prices come from official pages. Paid spots sit beside that content, labeled, and never inside the rankings or the price table.

What won't you run?

Anything we wouldn't tell a friend to use: “guaranteed AI income”, crypto or debt schemes, tools that hide what they do with people's data, or anything that pretends to be editorial.
