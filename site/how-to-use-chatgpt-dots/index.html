How to use ChatGPT dots: setup, first job and safe settings

Short answer: open chatgpt.com/dots (or Dots in the ChatGPT sidebar or app), follow the setup, set Custom rules so it asks before sending, buying or sharing, then give it one read-only job. A dot is an always-on agent with its own cloud computer that keeps working between conversations. It's included with ChatGPT Pro and is rolling out gradually to selected Plus users (18+, not in the EU/EEA, UK or Switzerland), plus Business and eligible Enterprise workspaces (OpenAI Help Center; ChatGPT Learn). It isn't on the free plan.

Updated Nov 25, 2026 · By BotsDoThat

Facts come from OpenAI's help pages, linked inline and checked Nov 25, 2026; plan prices are as reported by The Verge. Dots are changing fast.

What a ChatGPT dot is

  • An agent that keeps going. Give it a goal and context; it tracks progress, works out the next step and comes back with results or a decision for you (ChatGPT Learn).
  • Its own computer and browser in the cloud. Work continues while your devices are off. You can open its computer and select Take over to do a step yourself, such as a sign-in (ChatGPT Learn).
  • It can look at connected apps on its own. OpenAI says a dot can review connected information proactively and form memories from it, even when you haven't asked (OpenAI Help Center). That's why you connect only what a job needs.
  • One dot, many places. The same dot answers in ChatGPT, Slack or a call, with the same memory (ChatGPT Learn).

How to set up ChatGPT dots: 7 steps

  1. Check your plan and country. Dots are on ChatGPT Pro and rolling out gradually to selected Plus users, for people 18 or older outside the EU/EEA, the UK and Switzerland. Business Standard and Premium seats and eligible Enterprise, Edu and Healthcare workspaces also get them. If you're on Plus and don't see dots, the rollout hasn't reached you: OpenAI Support can't turn it on and restarting the app won't help.
  2. Open Dots. Use the latest ChatGPT app on iPhone or Android, the desktop app (Mac or Windows) or chatgpt.com/dots in a desktop browser. Mobile web isn't supported. On the phone there's a Dot tab once you have one; on desktop, open Dots from the sidebar.
  3. Create and name your dot. Follow the onboarding prompts. Its handle starts as @yourname-dot and changes when you name it (a dot named Alfred becomes @yourname-alfred). It starts with context from your ChatGPT memory.
  4. Set your rules before you connect anything. On mobile, open your dot's profile, then Customize, then Custom rules. For sending, buying and sharing, pick Ask before taking action or Hand off to you. Also tell it in plain words: “Show me drafts before you send anything.”
  5. Give it one read-only job. Describe the job, what it may use and what it must not do. Attach a file with +. Start with something that only reads and reports back, like the prompt below, and check the first result before you give it more.
  6. Connect only the app that job needs. Profile, then Customize, then Plugins (for example Gmail or Google Drive). Read each app's permissions first: permission to read isn't permission to send. Leave access to your own computer off unless the job needs local files; it starts turned off.
  7. Check its work and know the off switch. In the desktop app, open its profile to see In progress, Scheduled and Completed. Pause it from the ••• menu. Pausing doesn't stop separate Work or Codex tasks or scheduled tasks, so stop those in Activity and Scheduled too.

Paste this as your first job. It only reads, so it's a safe test:

First job for your dot (read-only) 
Job: each weekday at 8 AM [your time zone], check my email from the last 24 hours and my calendar for today.
Tell me: 1) what's on today, 2) emails that need a reply today, 3) anything that looks like a scam or a bill due this week.
Use only: my connected email and calendar, read-only.
Never: send, reply, delete, archive, book, buy or sign up for anything.
Ask me first: before any step that changes something or contacts anyone. Asking for a draft is not permission to send it.
If unsure: say what you're unsure about instead of guessing.
Result: at most 10 short bullets, most important first. Confirm the schedule and an end date (4 weeks from today).

Want a different job? Write it in the free bot instructions builder or the 6-part AI agent instructions template. Asking for a fixed time? OpenAI suggests including a time zone and an end date and asking the dot to confirm the schedule (ChatGPT Learn).

Custom rules: the four choices

On mobile: your dot's profile → Customize → Custom rules. Describe the action, then pick one (OpenAI Help Center):

  • Take action without asking. Only for read-only things you've watched it do right.
  • Take action if pre-approved. “Pre-approved” means you asked for that exact action in your message.
  • Ask before taking action. The safe default for sending, sharing and changing things.
  • Hand off to you. Best for payments and anything you can't undo.

Rules don't override OpenAI's built-in safeguards, and OpenAI warns a dot can still make mistakes when following them. Some steps, like changing a password, you always do yourself.

Good first jobs for a dot

  • Ongoing watching: have your bot grab an earlier appointment when one opens up or have your bot check on every package and chase the late ones.
  • Mornings: get a morning brief that pulls in the emails you need for each of today's meetings or have your bot catch calendar clashes hiding in your email.
  • Holidays: let your bot project-manage your holiday dinner or run the family Secret Santa with your bot.
  • Work: rebook all your clients after a surprise schedule change.
  • Make it better over time: teach your bot to remember what works, so it stops repeating mistakes.

Before you let it act: 6 rules

  1. Connect one app at a time. A connected app can be read proactively, and disconnecting it doesn't delete what the dot already learned.
  2. Keep sending and buying on “Ask before taking action”. OpenAI: asking for a draft doesn't authorize sending it.
  3. Never paste passwords into the chat. Use the private sign-in request or Take over to sign in yourself.
  4. Leave “your computer” access off unless a job needs local files. If you turn it on, Revoke access when you're done.
  5. Treat what it reads as untrusted. Emails and web pages can hide instructions meant for agents: keep hidden instructions in emails and web pages from steering your bot.
  6. Know that Pause isn't everything. Stop scheduled and delegated tasks separately, and remember stopping doesn't undo what's already done.

ChatGPT dots vs other agents

No dot yet? Free options: Tasks in Gemini (free in six countries) and Meta Muse (free with a usage limit). For one-off tasks inside ChatGPT, use ChatGPT Work (what replaced agent mode). Prefer an agent you run yourself and chat with on your phone? Connect Hermes Agent to Telegram. Side by side: ChatGPT dots vs Grok Bot vs Meta Muse.

Questions

Can I use ChatGPT dots for free?

No. Dots aren't on the free ChatGPT plan. They're on ChatGPT Pro, rolling out to selected Plus users, and on Business Standard, Business Premium and eligible Enterprise, Edu and Healthcare workspaces. Free personal agents you can try instead: Tasks in Gemini (free in six countries) and Meta Muse (free with a usage limit).

Do ChatGPT Plus users get dots?

Some do. The Verge reported on Nov 20, 2026 that a small number of Plus users are getting dots, and OpenAI's help page says the Plus rollout is gradual. If you don't see Dots in the sidebar or at chatgpt.com/dots, it hasn't reached your account; OpenAI Support can't enable it for you.

How much do ChatGPT dots cost?

There's no separate price for individuals: a dot comes with your plan. The Verge reports Plus at $20 a month and Pro from $100. For Enterprise, OpenAI says there's no fee for your dot through Jan 13, 2027, then flat-rate billing from Jan 14, 2027, and work a dot hands to ChatGPT Work or Codex is billed separately.

Why don't I see dots in ChatGPT?

Check that you're on the right account and workspace, then open chatgpt.com/dots. Common reasons: you're on Plus and the gradual rollout hasn't reached you, you're in the EU/EEA, UK or Switzerland, you're under 18, you're on mobile web (not supported), or your work admin hasn't enabled dots for your role.

Is ChatGPT dots available in the UK or EU?

Not yet. OpenAI's help page says dots aren't currently available in the European Economic Area (including the EU), the UK or Switzerland. Meta Muse launched in the UK on Nov 24, 2026 if you want a personal agent there.

What's the difference between ChatGPT and a dot?

ChatGPT answers when you ask. A dot is an always-on agent with its own cloud computer and browser: it keeps working between conversations, follows up as things change, runs scheduled checks and comes back to you for decisions. For a one-off task, ChatGPT Work is still the tool.

Can my dot text me, call me or have its own email?

You can call your dot with the phone button in its conversation, but it can't call you. Texting is a beta for a limited number of Pro users in the US, Canada and Mexico (iMessage or RCS, not SMS), and OpenAI says those texts aren't end-to-end encrypted because a provider, Linq, relays them. At launch a dot can use your personal email if you connect it, but it can't have its own address.

How do I stop or delete my dot?

Pause it from the ••• menu in its profile; that keeps its information. Delete (same menu) ends that dot and starts deleting its saved memories, and it can't be undone. Either way, stop scheduled tasks and Work or Codex tasks it started separately. Disconnecting an app doesn't delete what the dot already learned from it.
