About

About BotsDoThat

A live, sourced feed of real things people hand to AI agents (inbox and phone calls, shopping, travel, family, health, work, small business), with real popularity numbers, plain-English guides, copy-paste prompts, build-your-own-bot blueprints and tips to run your bots for less.

Where the use cases come from

We look for real people describing real jobs they handed to an AI agent, and we look in more than one place: posts on X and Reddit, Hacker News discussions, YouTube walkthroughs, open-source projects on GitHub, plus articles and official product pages. Every use case links to its sources, and a “Seen on” line shows which of those places it turned up in. When a post makes a claim about a result, we say whose claim it is.

How we measure popularity

  • Only real counts. X likes and reposts come from X's API; Reddit upvotes come from the post; Hacker News points and comments come from Hacker News's public search API; YouTube view counts come from YouTube search results (measured Oct 24, 2026) and GitHub stars from each project's GitHub page. Each number shows where it came from and the date we measured it.
  • Unknown stays unknown. If we couldn't measure it, we show no number at all. We never estimate or round up.
  • Heat (for “Trending now”) adds up every measured post about the use case from the last 60 days, wherever it was posted: X (likes + 2 × reposts), Reddit (upvotes) and Hacker News (points), each divided by weeks since that post. So a use case people are discussing on several sites ranks higher than one that's only hot on X. Newer posts with real engagement rise; old ones fade.
  • YouTube views and GitHub stars are shown on each page but don't count toward heat: a view or a star isn't the same kind of signal as a vote, and they build up over years.
  • Numbers are refreshed by a daily routine, so they change over time.

Who runs this

An editor (Nik Schultz) and a team of AI agents that find new use cases, refresh numbers and check links. A human approves what gets published.

How we make money

Clearly labeled sponsorships (see advertise), and possibly affiliate links in future, always labeled and explained on our disclosure page. Money never changes rankings: trending is computed from public numbers.
