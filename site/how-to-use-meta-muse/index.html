How to use Meta Muse: your first job, step by step

Short answer: open the Muse app or muse.ai, sign in with a Meta account, set Settings → Permissions to Always ask, then type one clear, low-risk job in the Chat tab. Muse plans it, works across the apps you connect and the web, and asks before important actions like sending an email or buying something. It's free with a usage limit; Power is $20 a month and Maximum $100 a month (Meta Help Center; Muse subscriptions). It launched in the UK on Nov 24, 2026 (Meta).

Updated Nov 25, 2026 · By BotsDoThat

Every fact comes from Meta's own Help Center and announcements, linked inline and checked Nov 25, 2026. Muse is changing fast and features vary by country.

What Meta Muse is

  • A personal AI agent, not just a chatbot. You give it a job; it turns it into a plan and works through it across connected apps and websites, opening a browser and filling in forms when needed (Meta).
  • It keeps working when you close the app. For longer jobs it comes back when something changes or when it needs your approval (Meta).
  • Its own computer in the cloud. Each Muse gets a virtual computer that stores the logins for the services you connect; Meta says Muse can't see your passwords or card details (Meta; Meta).
  • Connectors give it abilities. Connect Gmail or a calendar and it can read new emails and see schedule changes; it won't download your whole inbox unless you ask (Meta Help Center).

How to start with Meta Muse: 7 steps

  1. Check you can use it. Muse isn't available everywhere yet, and some features vary by country. Meta's launch posts include the UK (Nov 24, 2026), Australia (Nov 17) and Singapore (Oct 27). In the UK it's for anyone 18 or over, and you must be 18 or the age of majority to subscribe.
  2. Open Muse and sign in. Use the Muse app on iPhone or Android, or muse.ai in a browser (there's also a Mac app, and in the UK you can chat with Muse in WhatsApp). Sign in with a Meta account; if you don't have one, create it with Facebook, Instagram, an email address or a phone number.
  3. Set it to ask first. Before you connect anything, go to Settings, then Permissions. Set Connectors to Always ask and Web access defaults to Always ask while you learn. Muse will then ask before any action and before opening any website.
  4. Give it one low-risk job. Describe what you need clearly, say what it may use, and say what it must not do (Meta's own example: “Only search my email, not my text messages”). Start with a job that only reads and reports back. Copy the prompt below.
  5. Connect only the app that job needs. Ask “Connect my Gmail”, or go to Settings, then Connectors, tap Connect and follow the sign-in steps. Note that Facebook, Instagram and Threads connect automatically if they're in the same Accounts Center.
  6. Answer approvals with Allow once. When Muse asks, you can pick Allow once, Allow for this task, Allow for this site, Always allow or Deny. Use Allow once until you've seen it do the job right a few times. Tap See task details if you're not sure what it's about to do.
  7. Check what it did. Tap your assistant icon for the Activity log (every action and every permission you gave) and Upcoming (scheduled tasks and reminders). If something is wrong, tell it to stop or undo, or disconnect the app.

Paste this as your first job. It only reads, so it's a safe test:

First job for Meta Muse (read-only) 
Job: each weekday morning, look at my calendar for today and my email from the last 24 hours.
Tell me: 1) what's on today and when to leave, 2) emails that need a reply today, 3) anything that looks like a scam.
Use only: my calendar and email, read-only.
Never: send, reply, delete, archive, book, buy or sign up for anything.
Ask me first: before any step that changes something or contacts anyone.
If unsure: say what you're unsure about instead of guessing.
Result: at most 10 short bullets, most important first.

Permissions: what “Always ask” and “Allow once” mean

  • Read vs write. Reading is looking (your calendar, an email). Writing is changing or sending (a message, a purchase). Keep writing on approval (Meta Help Center).
  • Ask for some actions (Connectors): Muse asks before every write action and important read actions. Always ask: before any action.
  • Web access defaults: Ask for some actions = asks when your information may be shared or a site is unfamiliar; Always ask = asks before visiting any website.
  • Per-app and per-task: under Manage permissions you can set each Connector, approvals for each scheduled task and artifact, and revoke allowed websites.
  • “Always allow” is sticky. It lets Muse take that kind of action for that Connector from now on without asking. Save it for read-only jobs you've watched work.

Letting Muse pay for things

Muse always asks for your approval before it completes a purchase. Meta recommends Link by Stripe, which makes a one-time card number for each purchase so your real card number stays private; Muse can also log in to a shop with saved credentials and use your saved card. Check the total and the shop's terms first: you're responsible for what Muse buys, and it varies by country, shop and checkout (Meta Help Center). Good first jobs for Muse

  • Chores: hand your most-hated weekly chores to a personal agent.
  • Family: catch family calendar clashes before the week starts, turn the flood of school emails into one weekly digest or set up medication and appointment reminders for a parent (reminders are built in).
  • Inbox and news: get a short morning digest on topics you care about or have your bot catch calendar clashes hiding in your email.
  • Shopping: have your bot check on every package and chase the late ones or let an agent reorder your regular groceries.
  • Phone calls (a paid-plan allowance on Meta's help page): have your bot make a customer-service phone call for you. More: can AI make phone calls for you?

Before you let it act: 6 rules

  1. Start with low-risk jobs. Meta's own advice while you're learning (Meta Help Center).
  2. Never paste passwords or card numbers into the chat. Let Muse use its own sign-in store and Link by Stripe, or do that step yourself.
  3. Remember a sent email can't be unsent. Muse can stop or undo some actions, like reading a site or comparing prices, but not others.
  4. Be careful with custom connectors. Muse can build one for a service that isn't listed, but Meta doesn't review custom connectors (Meta Help Center).
  5. Treat what it reads as untrusted. Emails and web pages can hide instructions meant for the agent (prompt injection): keep hidden instructions in emails and web pages from steering your bot.
  6. Disconnecting isn't forgetting. After you disconnect an app, what Muse learned can stay in its memory and chat history, so ask it to forget too. Reset deletes everything, permanently.

Questions

Is Meta Muse free?

Yes. Muse is free with a usage limit. When you hit it, you can wait for it to refresh, buy a one-time pack of extra Muse tokens (they never expire), or subscribe: Power is $20 a month and Maximum is $100 a month on Meta's help page. Meta doesn't publish the free allowance as a number on its help pages.

How much does Meta Muse cost?

Nothing to start. Paid plans on Meta's help page: Power at $20 a month (500 million Muse tokens a week and 10 phone calls a day) and Maximum at $100 a month (3 billion tokens a week and 20 phone calls a day). Plans renew monthly until you cancel, and benefits can vary by region.

Is Meta Muse safe?

It's built to ask before important actions, such as sending an email or making a purchase, and Meta says it can't see your passwords or card details. But Meta's own help page warns that Muse can make mistakes or take unexpected actions, and that you're responsible for what it does for you. Start with low-risk jobs, set permissions to Always ask, and check the Activity log.

Is Meta Muse available in the UK?

Yes, from Nov 24, 2026, for anyone 18 or over, on iOS, Android, muse.ai and WhatsApp. It launched with UK partners such as Marks & Spencer, ASOS, Currys, easyJet and Jet2.

Can I use Muse in WhatsApp?

In the UK, yes: Meta says you can chat with Muse in the Muse app or directly in WhatsApp, with the same memory in both. Meta's Australia and Singapore announcements list iOS, Android and muse.ai, not WhatsApp.

Can Muse buy things for me?

Yes, with your approval every time. Meta recommends paying with Link by Stripe, which issues a one-time card number for each purchase so your real card stays private. Muse can also log in to a shop with credentials saved in its Secure Credential Store and pay with your saved card. Check the total and the shop's terms before you approve; you're responsible for the purchase.

Does Muse keep working after I close the app?

Yes, for longer tasks. Meta says Muse keeps working after you close the app and comes back when something changes or when it needs your approval, for example before it sends an email or makes a purchase. Each Muse has its own virtual computer in the cloud.

How do I stop Muse or delete what it knows?

Ask it to stop or undo in chat (some actions, like a sent email, can't be undone). Disconnect any app in Settings, then Connectors; what it learned may stay in its memory, so also ask it to forget. Resetting Muse permanently deletes all its information, chats, files and active tasks.
