  1. 💼 Work & career#1

    Build a second brain your bot reads every morning

    Keep your notes, plans and to-dos in plain files you own, plus one short "about me" file. Your bot reads them at the start of every chat, so it remembers who you are and picks up where you left off.

    • Starts every session by reading your "about me" file: who you are, how you like things done, what you're working on.
    • Answers questions from your own notes, like when the car last got new tires or what you gave your mom for her birthday.
    • Builds a short daily brief before you wake up from your notes, calendar and email.
    • Writes a summary of what it did and what it learned back into your notes at the end of each session.

    You end up with: An assistant that remembers your life and work across days, with all of that memory sitting in a folder you control.
