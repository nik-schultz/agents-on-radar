Top 9 this week

  1. ✈️ Bookings & travel#1

    Hand your agent a quick errand from your smartwatch

    Meta's Muse agent now has an Apple Watch and Wear OS app. Say or type a quick errand on your wrist, like finding movie tickets or fixing a calendar clash, and approve the result without pulling out your phone.

    • Takes your request from the watch by voice call, dictation or a typed message.
    • Does the work in the cloud with the apps and sites you connected (calendar, email, booking sites), even after you close the app.
    • Comes back with a suggestion, for example 'your dentist appointment clashes with a meeting, move it to 4pm?'.
    • Waits for you to tap approve before it books, sends or buys anything.

    You end up with: Small errands started from your wrist and finished in the background, with you approving the final step.

    Have your bot make a customer-service phone call for you

Grok Bot can place the call itself for US businesses (live transcript, take-over button, summary after), Meta's Muse can now do the same for paid US subscribers (from Nov 12, 2026), or you can still connect a phone-calling add-on.

  9. 💼 Work & career#9

    Keep an AI agent from deleting your files

    Before an agent like Claude Cowork, Codex, OpenClaw or Hermes works on your computer, give it one working folder of copies, keep a backup it can't reach, and turn on its read-only or sandbox setting, so one wrong command can't wipe your files.
