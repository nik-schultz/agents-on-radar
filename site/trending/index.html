  1. ✈️ Bookings & travel#1

    Give your bot a phone number so it can call and book for you

    Plenty of places still only take bookings by phone. Once your bot has its own number, it can call a restaurant, a vet or a salon, book the slot and tell you what was said.

    • Finds the business's number on its own.
    • Asks what it needs: how flexible you are on time, the name to book under, a callback number.
    • Reads the plan back and waits for your OK before it dials.
    • Makes the call, says it's an AI calling for you, and books.

    You end up with: A booking made by phone, a plain summary of the call and a calendar entry, without you making the call.
