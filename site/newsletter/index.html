Questions about the newsletter

Is the BotsDoThat Weekly free?

Yes. It's a free email. If an issue ever has a sponsor, the sponsor block is clearly labeled and it doesn't change what we pick.

How often will I get it?

One email a week, no more.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. Every job comes with a plain-English prompt you can paste into an AI app you already use, and the full guide on the site says what you end up with and what to watch out for.

How do I unsubscribe?

Every email has an unsubscribe link at the bottom. One click and you're off the list.

What happens to my email address?

We use it only to send the newsletter. Signups are double opt-in: you get a confirmation email and you aren't subscribed until you click it. We never sell or share it. Details: privacy.
