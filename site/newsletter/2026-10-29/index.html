Let AI shop, plan and sort for you (it asks first)

This week's picks are about AI that does things, not just answers: buying, booking, sorting and nudging. Each prompt below tells the AI to stop and ask you before any money moves or anything gets shared or deleted. Copy one, fill in the brackets, and try it.

1. Let your AI find and buy things for you with Shop Pay

Holiday lists are starting. Shopify's free Shop connection lets ChatGPT or Claude search stores and check out with Shop Pay, and you approve every purchase. Your card number never goes to the AI.

You end up with: The thing you wanted, found and ordered from one chat, with every purchase approved by you and the order tracked in your Shop app.

Copy this prompt 
Use the Shop connection to help me buy [what you want, e.g. a rain jacket for a 10-year-old]. My budget is [$] including shipping, and it needs to arrive by [date]. Only show items from stores that accept returns for at least 30 days. Give me your top 3 with the total price, delivery date and return window for each. Don't check out or pay for anything until I reply "buy number [1/2/3]".

Difficulty: Easy · Source: Shop Help Center: using Shop with AI agents (add the Shop skill to ChatGPT, Claude and others; Shop Pay in Muse, Instinct and Claude Duet; approvals, budgets and what gets shared) · Full guide with watch-outs

2. Add an AI to your friends' group chat to make plans happen

Friendsgiving or a weekend away stuck in a 60-message thread? Instinct's group-chat agent opened to all iMessage users on Oct 27. It collects dates and budgets, suggests options and books only when someone says so.

You end up with: A plan everyone agreed to, already booked, with the details pinned in the same chat where you talked about it.

Copy this prompt 
You're helping our group plan [a weekend trip to the lake / dinner on Friday]. Ask everyone in the chat for the dates and budget that work, suggest 2 or 3 options with prices, and keep a running list of who's in. Don't book or pay for anything until [I / the organiser] replies "book it", and post the confirmation number in the chat when it's done.

Difficulty: Easy · Source: TechCrunch (Oct 5, 2026): Instinct brings its AI agent to group chats, even for friends without an account; trip planning, tickets, carpools and Thanksgiving lists · Full guide with watch-outs

3. Organize your photos by just asking

After Halloween and fall trips, ask Gemini to pull the best shots into a private album. The prompt tells it to leave your originals alone. It's rolling out gradually, so you may not have it yet.

You end up with: Curated albums of your best photos from each trip or event, made by describing what you want, and kept private until you say to share them.

Copy this prompt 
Look in my Google Photos for pictures from [the trip, party or event, e.g. our beach trip last July]. Pick the best [15] photos and videos, leave out blurry shots and near-duplicates, and put them in a new private album called '[album name]'. Don't delete, move or edit any of my original photos, and don't share the album or email anyone. When you're done, tell me how many you added and list anything you weren't sure belonged, so I can check it.

Difficulty: Easy · Source: Gemini Apps Help: Streamline your Google Photos workflows in Gemini Apps · Full guide with watch-outs

4. Get a text from your bot before something needs doing

If your calendar runs your week, have an assistant watch it and text you only when something needs a decision, with one fix ready and quiet hours you choose.

You end up with: Fewer surprises: a heads-up text with the fix already lined up, so a yes is all it takes.

Copy this prompt 
Every morning at [7:30], look at my calendar and email for today and tomorrow. Text me only if something needs a decision: a delay, a clash, a reply someone is waiting for, or a free block I said I wanted to fill with [family time]. Offer one fix with the price. Don't book, buy or reply until I say yes, and never text me between [9 pm] and [7 am].

Difficulty: Easy · Source: Product Hunt (Oct 26, 2026): Kiwi – “A proactive AI assistant that texts you before you ask”, #11 product of the day · Full guide with watch-outs

One setting to check

Gemini API now saves requests by default for new paid projects

If you set up a new paid Gemini API project, the prompts your bot sends are now stored by default, matching Google's Interactions API; existing projects don't change. If you'd rather they weren't kept, send store=false or turn off Generate Content API logging under Logs and Datasets.
