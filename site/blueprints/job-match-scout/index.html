Job Match Scout

Get a daily shortlist of jobs that fit you, with notes. You apply yourself.

Medium4-step bot
Goal
Get a daily shortlist of jobs that fit you, with notes. You apply yourself.
Runs
⏱ Every weekday at 9:00am
Watches
👁 New listings on job boards that allow it

What it does, step by step

  1. Reads your CV and what you want once.
  2. Each weekday, checks new listings from the last 24 hours.
  3. Scores each 1–10 for fit and skips ones you've seen.
  4. Sends the 7+ ones with why they fit and a draft note.

Paste these starter instructions

Copy, paste into your tool, and replace the [brackets] with your details.

Starter instructions 
You are my Job Match Scout.
My CV: [paste]. I want: [titles], [location/remote], salary at least [X], not [things to avoid].
Every weekday at 9:00am, find roles posted in the last 24 hours. Skip any you've shown me before.
Score each 1–10 for fit. For 7 and above, send: title, company, link, why it fits, gaps, and a 3-sentence note to the hiring manager.
Rules: never apply, never create accounts, never invent experience I don't have.

What this costs to run

If you run this bot yourself on an AI API: our assumption is 22 runs a month, each reading about 20,000 tokens and writing about 800 (about 20 new job listings a weekday at roughly 1,000 tokens each). That's about 440,000 tokens in and 17,600 out a month.

How to set it up

  1. Pick one boring job. Choose ONE small job you do every week, like reading school emails or checking a web page.
  2. Pick your tool. Use what you already have.
  3. Paste the instructions and fill in the [brackets]. Open a new chat in the tool.
  4. Say “Run this once now and show me the result”. Don't schedule anything yet.
  5. Then say “Do this every [day/time]” and lock it down. Once the result looks right, ask it to repeat on a schedule.

Safety notes

  • Don't let it auto-apply: job sites block bots and can ban accounts.
  • Check every tailored CV for made-up claims.
