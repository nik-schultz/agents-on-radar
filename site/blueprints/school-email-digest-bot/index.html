School Email Digest

Never miss a permission slip, payment or deadline buried in school emails again.

MediumAbout 30 minutes (editor's estimate)
Goal
Never miss a permission slip, payment or deadline buried in school emails again.
Runs
⏱ Every Sunday at 6:00am
Watches
👁 Emails with your 'School' label (set up a filter first)

What it does, step by step

  1. In your email, make a filter that puts a 'School' label on everything from the school.
  2. The bot reads labeled emails it hasn't processed yet, including newsletter links.
  3. Pulls out deadlines, forms, payments and events for YOUR kids only.
  4. Sends a digest, urgent first, and labels each email 'Processed'.

Paste these starter instructions

Copy, paste into your tool, and replace the [brackets] with your details.

Starter instructions 
You are my School Email Digest bot.
Every Sunday at 6:00am, find emails labeled 'School' that are NOT labeled 'Processed'. Open any newsletter links inside them.
My kids: [initials], [grade], [school]. Ignore anything for other grades or for [activities we don't do].
Make a digest with these sections: 1) Closures and schedule changes, 2) Deadlines this week and next (forms, payments, sign-ups), 3) Events to put on the calendar, 4) Anything asking for a reply.
Remove duplicates. Put the date first on every line.
When done, add the label 'Processed' to each email you read.
Rules: draft replies to teachers but never send them. Don't share my kids' details anywhere else.

What this costs to run

If you run this bot yourself on an AI API: our assumption is 4 runs a month, each reading about 20,000 tokens and writing about 1,000 (a week of school email (about 40 messages) read once a week). That's about 80,000 tokens in and 4,000 out a month.

How to set it up

  1. Pick one boring job. Choose ONE small job you do every week, like reading school emails or checking a web page.
  2. Pick your tool. Use what you already have.
  3. Paste the instructions and fill in the [brackets]. Open a new chat in the tool.
  4. Say “Run this once now and show me the result”. Don't schedule anything yet.
  5. Then say “Do this every [day/time]” and lock it down. Once the result looks right, ask it to repeat on a schedule.

Safety notes

  • Use initials, not names, if you prefer.
  • Drafts only for anything going to the school.
  • Check the first two digests against the original emails.
