## "What subscribers get that the site doesn't" (new section on /newsletter/)

### What subscribers get that the site doesn't
Everything on botsdothat.com stays free and open. Subscribers also get:

- **The BotsDoThat Starter Pack (PDF).** 7 recurring agent setups, packaged as one guide. Each has the exact prompt, the setup steps for 4 tools, and what to do when a tool can't do it. It arrives the moment you confirm.
- **One subscriber-only extra every week.** It might be a ready-to-paste prompt set, a checklist or an upgraded setup. It's only in the email, not on the site or the web archive.
- **First word on new setups.** New jobs show up in the email before we write them up on the site.
- **A real reply.** Hit reply and tell us what broke. Fixes to the pack go out to subscribers first.
