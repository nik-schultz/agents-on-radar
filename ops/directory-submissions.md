### 1. Nukkadly — account required

- Submit page: https://nukkadly.com/submit
- Also useful: https://nukkadly.com/launch-your-product and https://nukkadly.com/faq
- Account: Yes. Create a free account, then submit. The submit page shows Log in / Submit product; the form is behind sign-in.
- Cost: Free review and a standard listing. Each UTC day has 10 free launch slots. The only paid option is a $39 one-time skip when a day is full. If the chosen day is full, pick another free day. Do not pay.
- Review: Human review, then the listing goes live on the UTC day you pick and stays in Browse after that week.
- Fit: Live software people can try today. Bot Checkup qualifies. Do not submit a waitlist-only page.
- After approval: complete any ownership verification they ask for with hello@botsdothat.com. Do not add a paid badge or Launch Pass.

Ready-to-submit copy:

- URL: https://botsdothat.com/bot-checkup/
- Additional site URL (if asked): https://botsdothat.com/
- Email: hello@botsdothat.com
- Categories (adjust to their picker): AI tools, Productivity, Privacy / Security
- Pricing: Free
- Short description / tagline: A free checkup that shows what your AI assistants can do with your accounts, and the exact settings to change in each app.
- Long description: Bot Checkup is a free, three-minute review for Grok Bot, ChatGPT dots, Claude Duet, Meta Muse and Gemini Tasks. You pick which assistants you use and what each one is connected to, then set each action to fine alone, ask first, or never.
